Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Central Drugs Standard Control Organization (CDSCO)

  • Nodal Agency: Statutory body under the Directorate General of Health Services, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare.
  • Governing Act: Enforces regulations under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and Rules, 1945.
  • Key Functionary: Headed by the Drugs Controller General of India (DCGI); headquartered in New Delhi.

📌 World Trade Organization (WTO)

  • Formation: Established on January 1, 1995, as a result of the 1994 Marrakesh Agreement, replacing the 1947 GATT.
  • Headquarters: Geneva, Switzerland; current membership stands at 164 member states.
  • Highest Body: The Ministerial Conference, which meets biennially to make major decisions regarding multilateral trade agreements.

📌 Section 22A of the Registration Act, 1908

  • Legal Mandate: Empowers state governments to prohibit registration of documents relating to transfer of public, endowment, or protected lands.
  • Objective: Prevents fraudulent alienation of government properties and ceiling surplus land.
  • Constitutional Context: 'Land' and 'Rights in or over land' fall under List II (State List) of the Seventh Schedule of the Indian Constitution.

📌 India-Nepal Energy Cooperation Framework

  • Foundational Agreement: Power Exchange Agreement signed between India and Nepal in 1971.
  • Transmission Asset: The 400 kV Dhalkebar-Muzaffarpur cross-border line acts as the primary transmission artery for high-capacity trade.
  • Nodal Entities: Grid Controller of India Limited (Grid-India) and Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA).

📌 El Niño-Southern Oscillation (ENSO)

  • Mechanism: Periodic warming of Sea Surface Temperatures (SST) in the central and eastern tropical Pacific Ocean.
  • Impact on India: Weakens the pressure gradient required for monsoon winds, frequently resulting in below-normal rainfall in South Asia.
  • Opposite Phase: La Niña, characterized by cooling SSTs in the Pacific, typically correlates with strong monsoon rainfall in India.
