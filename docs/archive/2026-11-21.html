Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Index of Core Industries (ICI) in India

  • Comprises eight core sector industries: Coal, Crude Oil, Natural Gas, Refinery Products, Fertilizers, Steel, Cement, and Electricity.
  • Refinery products have the highest weightage among the eight core industries in the ICI basket.
  • Compiled and released monthly by the Office of the Economic Adviser, Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT).

📌 Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO)

  • Statutory body established under the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act of 1952.
  • Functions under the administrative control of the Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India.
  • Administers mandatory provident fund, pension scheme, and insurance scheme for workforce participants in the organized sector.

📌 Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries (OPEC)

  • Founded in Baghdad in September 1960 by five founding members: Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, and Venezuela.
  • Headquarters moved to Vienna, Austria, in 1965.
  • Aims to coordinate and unify petroleum policies among member nations and secure fair and stable prices for petroleum producers.

📌 Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)

  • Statutory autonomous body established under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006.
  • Headquartered in New Delhi, operating under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.
  • Responsible for protecting and promoting public health through regulation and supervision of food safety standards across India.

📌 India Meteorological Department (IMD)

  • Established in 1875, it is the national meteorological service of the country and the principal agency in all matters relating to meteorology.
  • Operates under the Ministry of Earth Sciences, Government of India, with headquarters in New Delhi.
  • Responsible for meteorological observations, weather forecasting, and seismological tracking in the South Asian region.
