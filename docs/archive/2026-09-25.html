Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)

  • Launched in: 2020 as part of the Aatmanirbhar Bharat economic stimulus package.
  • Financing Structure: Provides interest subvention of 3% per annum up to a loan amount of ₹2 crore.
  • Target Beneficiaries: Farmers, Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS), Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), and agri-entrepreneurs.

📌 Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)

  • Statutory Basis: Established under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006.
  • Parent Ministry: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India.
  • Headquarters: New Delhi, responsible for laying down science-based standards for food articles.

📌 Sovereign Credit Rating Agencies

  • The 'Big Three' Agencies: Standard & Poor's (S&P), Moody's Ratings, and Fitch Ratings.
  • Function: Assess the creditworthiness of sovereign nations, influencing foreign institutional investment borrowing costs.
  • Key Metrics Evaluated: GDP growth trajectory, fiscal deficit, inflation rate, and external debt burden.

📌 State Disaster Response Fund (SDRF)

  • Statutory Basis: Constituted under Section 48(1)(a) of the Disaster Management Act, 2005.
  • Funding Pattern: Central government contributes 75% for general states and 90% for northeastern/himalayan states.
  • Utilization: Primary fund available with state governments for immediate relief during notified disasters like drought, floods, and cyclones.

📌 Preferential Trade Agreement (PTA)

  • Definition: A trading bloc that gives preferential access to certain products from participating countries by reducing tariffs.
  • Scope: Broader than a unilateral trade preference, but typically more limited in scope than a Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA) or Free Trade Agreement (FTA).
  • WTO Compliance: Permitted under WTO enabling clauses for developing economies seeking regional trade integration.
