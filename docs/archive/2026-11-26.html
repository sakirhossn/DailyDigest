📌 Economy & Banking

1. RBI Retains SBI, HDFC Bank, and ICICI Bank as Domestic Systemically Important Banks (D-SIBs)

Banking Awareness / RBI Policy
  • The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) maintained the status of State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, and ICICI Bank as Domestic Systemically Important Banks (D-SIBs) for the 2026 evaluation period.
  • D-SIBs are financial institutions deemed 'too big to fail', requiring additional Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital absorption buffers ranging from 0.20% to 0.80% of Risk-Weighted Assets to insulate the financial system against systemic risk.
💡 Static GK / Core Concept: The D-SIB framework was introduced by the RBI in 2014 following Global Systemically Important Banks (G-SIBs) guidelines set by the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS).

2. CPI Inflation Surges to 6.1% in October 2026 Driven by Food Price Pressures: RBI Bulletin

Economy / Inflation & Monetary Policy
  • According to the latest RBI Bulletin, Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation rose to 6.1% year-on-year in October 2026 from 5.5% in September, breaching the upper tolerance limit of 6% set by the Monetary Policy Framework.
  • Persistent price spikes in vegetables, pulses, and edible oils drove food inflation higher, constraining monetary policy headroom ahead of upcoming Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) reviews.
💡 Static GK / Core Concept: India's Flexible Inflation Targeting (FIT) framework mandates the RBI to maintain headline CPI inflation at 4% with an allowable tolerance band of +/- 2% (2% to 6%).

1. Government Allows Duty-Free Raw Sugar Imports up to 10 Lakh Tonnes till March 2027

Government Policy / SSC & State PSC
  • The Central Government authorized duty-free imports of raw sugar up to a ceiling of 10 lakh tonnes through March 2027 to stabilize domestic supply and curb retail price volatility.

💡 Static GK / Core Concept: The Sugar Marketing Season in India runs from October 1 to September 30 of the following year. Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) of sugarcane is fixed by the Central Government under the Sugarcane (Control) Order, 1966.

Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Domestic Systemically Important Banks (D-SIBs) Framework

  • Introduced by the RBI in 2014, requiring evaluation of banks based on size, interconnectedness, lack of substitutability, and complexity.
  • D-SIBs are placed in five buckets; higher buckets demand additional Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital requirements.
  • Currently, State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, and ICICI Bank are designated as India's D-SIBs.

📌 Border Roads Organisation (BRO)

  • Established on May 7, 1960, under the executive control of the Ministry of Defence to develop and maintain road networks in border areas.
  • Headquartered in New Delhi, BRO operates major overseas construction projects such as Project Dantak in Bhutan.
  • Motto: 'Sramena Sarvam Sadhyam' (Everything is achievable through hard work).

📌 Article 25 of the Indian Constitution

  • Guarantees freedom of conscience and the right to freely profess, practise, and propagate religion to all persons in India.
  • Subject to public order, morality, and health, as well as other provisions of Part III of the Constitution.
  • Empowers the State to regulate secular activities associated with religious practices and provide for social welfare and reform.

📌 PM-POSHAN Scheme (Mid-Day Meal Scheme)

  • Originally launched in 1995 as the National Programme of Nutritional Support to Primary Education, renamed PM-POSHAN in 2021.
  • Covers eligible school children studying in Classes I to VIII in government and government-aided schools.
  • Nodal Ministry: Ministry of Education, funded on a joint sharing basis between Central and State governments.

📌 Service Producer Price Index (PPI)

  • Measures the average change in selling prices received by domestic service producers for their output.
  • Compiled by the Office of the Economic Adviser (OEA), Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT).
  • Serves as a critical deflator for calculating National Accounts Statistics and GDP growth in the service sector.
