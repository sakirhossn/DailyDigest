Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Foreign Exchange Reserves Management in India

  • Governed under Section 45CC of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
  • Held and managed by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to maintain orderly conditions in foreign exchange markets.
  • Comprises Foreign Currency Assets (FCAs), Gold reserves, Special Drawing Rights (SDRs), and IMF reserve tranches.

📌 Strait of Hormuz Geopolitical Significance

  • A critical maritime chokepoint situated between Oman and Iran, linking the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea.
  • Vital transit corridor for nearly a fifth of the world's petroleum consumption and global liquefied natural gas shipments.
  • High strategic relevance for global energy security and crude oil price stability.

📌 International Energy Agency (IEA) and Strategic Petroleum Reserves

  • Established in 1974 in the wake of the 1973 oil crisis, headquartered in Paris, France.
  • Mandates member countries to hold emergency oil stocks equivalent to at least 90 days of net oil imports.
  • India became an Associate member of the IEA in March 2017 to strengthen global energy dialogue.

📌 Extradition Laws and Treaties in India

  • Regulated primarily by the Indian Extradition Act, 1962.
  • Defines the legal framework for surrendering fugitives to foreign states or requesting custody of offenders from treaty partners.
  • Requires dual criminality, meaning the offense must be punishable under the laws of both contracting nations.

📌 Padma Shri and Arjuna Awards in Sports

  • Arjuna Award was instituted in 1961 by the Government of India to recognize outstanding achievement in national sports.
  • Padma Shri is India's fourth-highest civilian award, conferred for distinguished service in various fields including sports and public service.
  • Pargat Singh is an acclaimed former Indian field hockey captain who received both prestigious accolades for his monumental sporting career.
