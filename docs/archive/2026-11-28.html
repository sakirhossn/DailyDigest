Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) in Indian Banking

  • Mandated under Section 42(1) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
  • Commercial banks must maintain a specified percentage of their Net Demand and Time Liabilities (NDTL) with the RBI.
  • Does not earn any interest for the scheduled commercial banks.

📌 Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)

  • Established under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006.
  • Functions under the administrative purview of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.
  • Headquartered in New Delhi.

📌 Indian Naval Academy (INA), Ezhimala

  • Asia's largest naval academy, situated in the Kannur district of Kerala.
  • Commissioned to impart comprehensive professional training to naval cadets.
  • Nestled between the Kavvayi backwaters and the Arabian Sea.

📌 Consumer Price Index (CPI) Inflation in India

  • Measures retail inflation by collecting price data for a basket of goods and services consumed by retail households.
  • Compiled and published monthly by the National Statistical Office (NSO), MoSPI.
  • The primary index utilized by the RBI for its flexible inflation targeting mandate.

📌 Bilateral Trade Agreements

  • International treaties between two countries aiming to liberalize trade restrictions and customs duties.
  • Designed to expand market access and enhance economic cooperation.
  • Governed internationally under World Trade Organization (WTO) provisions and regional trade pact frameworks.
