Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)

  • Established under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, replacing multiple older food laws like the Prevention of Food Adulteration Act, 1954.
  • Operates under the administrative jurisdiction of the Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare; headquarters in New Delhi.
  • Empowered to lay down science-based standards for food items and regulate manufacture, storage, distribution, and import to ensure wholesome food for human consumption.

📌 PM-KUSUM Scheme Architecture

  • Launched in 2019 by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) to provide energy and water security to farmers while de-dieselizing irrigation.
  • Component A: Setting up of 10,000 MW decentralized ground-mounted grid-connected solar power plants on barren or agricultural land.
  • Component B & C: Installation of 20 lakh standalone solar agriculture pumps (B) and solarization of 15 lakh grid-connected agriculture pumps (C).

📌 Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

  • Constituted as a non-statutory body in 1988 and conferred full statutory powers on April 12, 1992, through the SEBI Act, 1992.
  • Headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra, with regional offices in Ahmedabad, Kolkata, New Delhi, and Chennai.
  • Governed by a Board comprising a Chairman and members nominated by the Central Government, RBI, and Ministry of Finance.

📌 Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In)

  • Operationalized in 2004 under the Department of Information Technology; designated as national nodal agency under Section 70B of the Information Technology Act, 2000.
  • Functions under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) to handle cybersecurity threats, vulnerabilities, and data incident response.
  • Mandates all service providers, intermediaries, and corporate entities to report critical cybersecurity incidents within a stipulated timeframe.

📌 National Policy for Rare Diseases (NPRD) 2021

  • Formulated by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare to categorize rare genetic disorders into three groups based on treatment feasibility and cost.
  • Group 1 disorders require one-time curative treatment; Group 2 requires long-term/lifelong therapy with lower costs; Group 3 requires high-cost lifelong therapy (e.g., SMA, Gaucher's disease).
  • Designates premier tertiary government institutions as Centers of Excellence (CoEs) for diagnosis, prevention, and treatment facilitation.
