Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA)

  • Established in August 2003 by the Government of India and later given statutory status via the PFRDA Act, 2013.
  • Headquarters: New Delhi, India.
  • Regulates the National Pension System (NPS) and Atal Pension Yojana (APY) to secure old-age income security for citizens.

📌 National Investment and Infrastructure Fund (NIIF)

  • Established in 2015 as India’s first sovereign-backed investment fund to provide long-term capital for core economic infrastructure.
  • Headquarters: Mumbai, Maharashtra.
  • Operates through three distinct funds: Master Fund, Fund of Funds, and Strategic Opportunities Fund.

📌 International Monetary Fund (IMF) and World Economic Outlook

  • Created in 1944 at the Bretton Woods Conference alongside the World Bank, commencing operations in December 1945.
  • Headquarters: Washington, D.C., United States.
  • Publishes flagship economic reports including the World Economic Outlook (WEO) and Global Financial Stability Report (GFSR).

📌 Madras High Court and Article 226 of the Constitution

  • Established in 1862 under the Indian High Courts Act 1861, making it one of the three chartered high courts in India.
  • Jurisdiction extends over the state of Tamil Nadu and the Union Territory of Puducherry.
  • Empowered under Article 226 to issue writs for the enforcement of fundamental rights and other legal remedies.

📌 Article 190 of the Indian Constitution

  • Governs the vacation of seats in State Legislatures in India.
  • Deals with simultaneous membership across houses, absence from sittings without permission, and resignation of seats by members.
  • Requires resignations to be submitted in writing to the Speaker or Chairman, who must accept them after verifying genuineness.
