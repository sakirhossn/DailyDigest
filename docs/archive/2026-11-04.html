Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Goods and Services Tax (GST) Framework in India

  • Introduced via the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016.
  • It is a destination-based comprehensive indirect tax levied on the manufacture, sale, and consumption of goods and services.
  • Governed by the GST Council chaired by the Union Finance Minister.

📌 Urban Cooperative Banks (UCBs)

  • Referred to loosely as cooperative banks located in urban and semi-urban areas.
  • Historically, they were organized on a cooperative basis and are regulated jointly by the RBI (under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949) and State Registrars of Cooperative Societies.
  • Recent policy updates by RBI focus on strengthening their auditing standards and multi-state cooperative compliance.

📌 Prakasam Barrage and Krishna River

  • Prakasam Barrage stretches across the Krishna River in Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh.
  • The Krishna River is the second-east-flowing interstate river of peninsular India, originating near Mahabaleshwar in Maharashtra.
  • Major tributaries of the Krishna include the Tungabhadra, Bhima, Musi, and Munneru rivers.

📌 Viability Gap Funding (VGF)

  • A financial grant provided by the government to bridge the viability gap of infrastructure projects that are economically sound but commercially unviable.
  • Administered under specific central sector schemes to attract private sector participation in public infrastructure.
  • Frequently utilized in renewable energy, grid-scale storage, and transport projects.

📌 NMDC Limited (National Mineral Development Corporation)

  • Established in 1958, it is a CPSE under the Ministry of Steel, Government of India.
  • It is India's largest iron ore producer and exporter, operating major mechanized mines in Chhattisgarh and Karnataka.
  • Headquartered in Hyderabad, Telangana.
