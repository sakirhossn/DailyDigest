Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Ministry of Textiles and Export Promotion Councils

  • Headquarters: New Delhi, India.
  • Responsible for policy formulation, export promotion, and skill development across the entire textile value chain.
  • Oversees various Export Promotion Councils (EPCs) such as AEPC and TEXPROCIL.

📌 World Bank Group Composition

  • Comprises five distinct institutions: IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, and ICSID.
  • Established in 1944 following the Bretton Woods Agreement.
  • Headquartered in Washington, D.C., USA, with the primary objective of reducing global poverty.

📌 Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs) in India

  • Operate under the Directorate General of Training (DGT), Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship.
  • Provide vocational training across various engineering and non-engineering trades to school leavers.
  • Designed to supply skilled technical personnel to domestic industries.

📌 Kambala Traditional Sport

  • Traditional buffalo race conducted annually in the coastal Karnataka region (Tulunadu).
  • Held in slushy, water-filled paddy fields amidst lively traditional music and large crowds.
  • Recognised as an integral element of rural folk culture and heritage preservation.

📌 Mysuru Dasara (Nada Habba)

  • State festival of Karnataka celebrating the victory of Goddess Chamundeshwari over Mahishasura.
  • Features the iconic 'Jamboo Savari' grand elephant procession starting from the Mysuru Palace.
  • Marks the culmination of nine nights of Navaratri festivities.
