Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)

  • Launched on April 8, 2015, under the Ministry of Finance to provide non-collateralized micro-loans up to ₹10 lakh to non-corporate micro/small enterprises.
  • Loans are extended by Commercial Banks, RRBs, Small Finance Banks, MFIs, and NBFCs, with guarantee coverage provided by NCGTC.
  • Categorized into Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (above ₹50,000 to ₹5 lakh), and Tarun (above ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh).

📌 Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS) Scheme

  • Implemented by the Department of Fertilizers, Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers, effective from April 1, 2010.
  • Applies to P&K (Phosphatic and Potassic) fertilizers where a fixed annual/bi-annual subsidy per kg is announced for Nitrogen (N), Phosphate (P), Potash (K), and Sulphur (S).
  • Urea remains outside NBS and continues under statutory MRP administration.

📌 Purchasing Managers' Index (PMI)

  • An economic indicator derived from monthly surveys of private sector companies assessing business activity, new orders, employment, and prices.
  • A reading above 50 indicates expansion relative to the previous month, below 50 indicates contraction, and exactly 50 represents no change.
  • In India, Flash and Final PMI figures for Manufacturing and Services are compiled by S&P Global / HSBC.

📌 Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015

  • Enacted to replace the Juvenile Justice Act, 2000, aligning Indian law with the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child.
  • Mandates the constitution of a Juvenile Justice Board (JJB) comprising a Judicial Magistrate First Class and two social workers in every district.
  • Section 15 permits preliminary assessment by the JJB for heinous offences alleged to be committed by children between 16 and 18 years of age.

📌 International Film Festival of India (IFFI)

  • Established in 1952, it is conducted annually in Goa under the aegis of the National Film Development Corporation (NFDC) and Ministry of Information and Broadcasting.
  • The highest award conferred at IFFI is the 'Golden Peacock' for Best Film, carrying a trophy and cash prize.
  • Recognized by FIAPF (International Federation of Film Producers Associations) as a competitive feature film festival.
