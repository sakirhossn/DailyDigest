Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Article 293 of the Indian Constitution (Borrowing by States)

  • Clause (1) empowers the executive of a State to borrow within the territory of India upon the security of the Consolidated Fund of the State.
  • Clause (3) mandates central consent if a State has any outstanding central loan or guaranteed obligation.
  • It acts as a primary constitutional check ensuring national macroeconomic stability and debt sustainability.

📌 National Statistical Office (NSO)

  • Created in May 2019 by merging the Central Statistics Office (CSO) and the National Sample Survey Office (NSSO).
  • Operates under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) headed by a Director General.
  • Responsible for releasing key economic barometers including GDP estimates, IIP, and Consumer Price Index (CPI).

📌 Panchayati Raj Constitutional Framework (73rd Amendment)

  • Passed in 1992 and came into force on April 24, 1993, observed annually as National Panchayati Raj Day.
  • Mandated a three-tier structure: Gram Panchayat (village), Panchayat Samiti (block), and Zilla Parishad (district).
  • Article 243D provides mandatory reservation of not less than one-third of total seats for women.

📌 Consolidated Sinking Fund (CSF)

  • Instituted by the Reserve Bank of India in 1999-2000 to assist State Governments in amortizing their debt obligations.
  • States contribute a specified percentage of their outstanding liabilities annually to build a debt redemption reserve.
  • The fund is managed by the Internal Debt Management Department of the Reserve Bank of India.

📌 WMO/ESCAP Panel on Tropical Cyclones (PTC)

  • Established in 1972 under the joint auspices of the World Meteorological Organization and UN ESCAP.
  • Currently comprises 13 members: India, Bangladesh, Maldives, Myanmar, Oman, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Iran, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Yemen.
  • IMD serves as the Regional Specialized Meteorological Centre (RSMC) in New Delhi to track and issue cyclone advisories for this basin.
