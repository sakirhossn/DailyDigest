Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Flexible Inflation Targeting (FIT) in India

  • Introduced in 2016 following recommendations of the Urjit Patel Committee.
  • Statutorily anchored under Section 45ZA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
  • Target consumer price inflation (CPI-Combined) is fixed at 4% with a tolerance band of +/- 2%.

📌 State Legislative Council (Vidhan Parishad)

  • Governed under Article 169 and Article 171 of the Indian Constitution.
  • It is the upper house of a bicameral state legislature and is a permanent body not subject to dissolution.
  • One-third of its members are elected by electorates consisting of graduates residing in the state.

📌 Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) Regulation in India

  • Regulated primarily under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) of 1999.
  • Inflows and outflows are monitored and compiled by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and DPIIT.
  • Classified into automatic route and government approval route depending on the sector.

📌 National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013

  • Provides legal entitlement to subsidized food grains to up to 75% of the rural population and 50% of the urban population.
  • Distributes food grains at highly subsidized rates through the Targeted Public Distribution System (TPDS).
  • Includes special provisions for nutritional support to pregnant women, lactating mothers, and children.

📌 Office of the United States Trade Representative (USTR)

  • An agency of the United States federal government responsible for developing and recommending trade policy.
  • Cabinet-level agency directly reporting to the President of the United States.
  • Responsible for conducting trade negotiations and administering Section 301 investigations.
