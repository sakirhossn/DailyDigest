Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG)

  • Established under Article 148 of the Constitution, functioning as the head of the Indian Audit and Accounts Department.
  • Appointed by the President of India by warrant under his hand and seal for a term of 6 years or up to 65 years of age.
  • Acts as an agent of Parliament and conducts financial audits of all receipts and expenditure of the Government of India and State Governments.

📌 Kaziranga National Park

  • Located in the State of Assam, spanning across the floodplains of the Brahmaputra River.
  • Declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1985 and recognized as an Important Bird Area by BirdLife International.
  • World's largest natural habitat for the endangered Great Indian One-Horned Rhinoceros, alongside significant tiger and elephant populations.

📌 Consumer Price Index (CPI) in India

  • Compiled and published monthly by the National Statistical Office (NSO) under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI).
  • Measures changes in the price level of a market basket of consumer goods and services purchased by households.
  • Serves as the primary anchor index for inflation targeting by the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) of the RBI.

📌 International Monetary Fund (IMF)

  • Headquartered in Washington, D.C., United States, founded in 1944 at the Bretton Woods Conference.
  • Primary objective includes fostering global monetary cooperation, securing financial stability, facilitating international trade, and reducing poverty.
  • Issues an international reserve asset known as Special Drawing Rights (SDRs) to member countries.

📌 United States Commission on International Religious Freedom (USCIRF)

  • Established as an independent, bipartisan U.S. federal government commission by the International Religious Freedom Act of 1998.
  • Mandated to monitor the universal right to freedom of religion or belief abroad and make policy recommendations to the President and Congress.
  • Operates independently from the U.S. State Department, though its reports often influence international diplomatic discourse.
