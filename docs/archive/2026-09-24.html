Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

  • Established on April 12, 1992, under the SEBI Act, 1992.
  • Headquarters: Mumbai, Maharashtra.
  • Statutory regulatory body established to protect investors in securities and to regulate the securities market in India.

📌 Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)

  • Founded in 1961, succeeding the Organisation for European Economic Co-operation (OEEC).
  • Headquarters: Paris, France.
  • Comprises developed and emerging economies dedicated to promoting policies designed to achieve sustainable economic growth and employment.

📌 National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences (NIMHANS)

  • Premier institute for mental health and neurosciences in India located in Bengaluru.
  • Operates autonomously under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India.
  • Designated as an Institute of National Importance via parliamentary legislation.

📌 Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries (OPEC)

  • Established in Baghdad in September 1960 by founding members Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, and Venezuela.
  • Headquarters: Vienna, Austria.
  • Permanent intergovernmental organization aimed at coordinating and unifying petroleum policies among member countries.

📌 Asian Games & Olympic Council of Asia (OCA)

  • Governing body of sports in Asia, recognized by the International Olympic Committee (IOC).
  • Headquarters: Kuwait City, Kuwait.
  • Organizes the Asian Games quadrennial multi-sport event featuring athletes from all over Asia.
