Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Code on Social Security, 2020

  • Enacted to amalgamate 9 major central labour laws, including the Employees' Provident Funds Act, 1952, and the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961.
  • Introduced statutory recognition of 'gig workers' and 'platform workers' for the first time in Indian legislative history.
  • Mandates the constitution of a National Social Security Board chaired by the Union Minister for Labour and Employment.

📌 Article 215 of the Indian Constitution

  • Declares that every High Court shall be a Court of Record and shall have all the powers of such a court, including the power to punish for contempt of itself.
  • A Court of Record maintains proceedings whose acts and judicial proceedings are enrolled for perpetual memory and testimony, holding unquestioned evidentiary value.
  • Parallel provisions for the Supreme Court of India are contained in Article 129.

📌 Singareni Collieries Company Limited (SCCL)

  • Established originally in 1920 (originating as Hyderabad Deccan Company in 1889), headquartered at Kothagudem, Telangana.
  • Joint venture between the Government of Telangana (51% equity shareholding) and the Government of India (49% equity shareholding).
  • Operates exclusively along the Godavari Valley Coalfield, the only repository of proven coal reserves in South India.

📌 Department of Investment and Public Asset Management (DIPAM)

  • Originally set up as the Department of Disinvestment in December 1999 and subsequently renamed as DIPAM in April 2016.
  • Functions as one of the six nodal departments under the Ministry of Finance, Government of India.
  • Oversees the strategic sale of CPSE equity, asset monetization, and capital restructuring guidelines (including dividend policies).

📌 Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In)

  • Established in 2004 as the national agency for incident response, operating under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).
  • Empowered under Section 70B of the Information Technology Act, 2000 to collect, analyse, and disseminate information on cyber incidents.
  • Mandates mandatory reporting of specified cybersecurity incidents by service providers and corporate entities within strict regulatory timeframes.
