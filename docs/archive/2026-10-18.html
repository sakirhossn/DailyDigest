Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005

  • Enacted by the Parliament of India, coming fully into force on 12 October 2005, giving statutory expression to Article 19(1)(a) freedom of speech and expression.
  • Under Section 7(1), public information officers must provide information within 30 days of receiving the request (48 hours if it concerns the life or liberty of a person).
  • Section 8(1) sets forth categorical exemptions from disclosure, including sovereign security, commercial confidence, and personal privacy without overriding public interest.

📌 Northeast Monsoon (Retreating Monsoon) & IMD Framework

  • Originates due to the southward shift of the Inter-Tropical Convergence Zone (ITCZ) during October to December, blowing from northeast to southwest.
  • Crucial for the southern peninsula, specifically Tamil Nadu, coastal Andhra Pradesh, Rayalaseema, and south interior Karnataka, replenishing major reservoirs.
  • The India Meteorological Department (IMD), established in 1875 and headquartered in New Delhi, serves as the nodal forecasting agency under the Ministry of Earth Sciences.

📌 MSME Composite Classification Criteria (2020 Framework)

  • Effective 1 July 2020, India replaced the segregated manufacturing and services categorization with a composite threshold of Investment in Plant & Machinery/Equipment and Annual Turnover.
  • Micro: Investment ≤ ₹1 crore and Turnover ≤ ₹5 crore; Small: Investment ≤ ₹10 crore and Turnover ≤ ₹50 crore.
  • Medium: Investment ≤ ₹50 crore and Turnover ≤ ₹250 crore; exports of goods and services are excluded from the turnover calculation.

📌 Directorate General of Training (DGT) and Vocational Framework

  • DGT functions as the principal policy-making body for vocational training and apprenticeships under the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE).
  • It manages the Craftsmen Training Scheme (CTS) implemented through government and private Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs).
  • National Council for Vocational Education and Training (NCVET) regulates and oversees national vocational qualifications, ensuring standardization across awarding bodies.

📌 International Development Association (IDA) - World Bank Group

  • Established in 1960 as the soft-loan window of the World Bank Group, headquartered in Washington, D.C.
  • Provides grants and zero to low-interest concessional credits (with maturities extending up to 30–40 years) to the world's 75 poorest developing nations.
  • Replenished every three years by donor partner countries; India successfully graduated from IDA borrower status in 2014 and is now an IDA donor.
