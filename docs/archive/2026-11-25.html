Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 State Development Loans (SDLs)

  • Issued by state governments in India to finance their fiscal deficits and developmental expenditure.
  • Conducted via auctions managed by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) through the E-Kuber platform.
  • Considered sovereign-equivalent safety instruments carrying attractive yields compared to central government securities.

📌 John Doe Orders in Indian Law

  • Also known as Ashok Kumar orders, originating from English common law traditions.
  • An ex-parte interim injunction issued against anonymous entities to prevent large-scale piracy, copyright violation, or public property damage.
  • Enforced widely by High Courts in intellectual property infringement and digital censorship suits.

📌 Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007

  • A comprehensive central legislation enacted to mandate financial maintenance for parents and senior citizens by children or relatives.
  • Establishes Maintenance Tribunals headed by Sub-Divisional Officers for speedy adjudication.
  • Includes provisions where property transfers made by senior citizens can be declared void if the transferee neglects basic physical and financial needs.

📌 Customs Act, 1962 and Customs Tariff Act, 1975

  • The primary legislative statutes governing import and export duties, anti-dumping measures, and tariff classifications in India.
  • Administered by the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) under the Ministry of Finance.
  • Incorporates Harmonized System of Nomenclature (HSN) codes for international commodity classification.

📌 Fertilizer Subsidy Regime in India

  • Managed predominantly through the Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS) scheme and fixed maximum retail price (MRP) controls for urea.
  • Aims to ensure affordable nutrient availability to farmers despite global price volatility.
  • Directly impacts the fiscal deficit of the Union Government through allocations managed by the Department of Fertilizers.
