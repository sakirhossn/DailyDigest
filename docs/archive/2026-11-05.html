Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI)

  • Responsible for the formulation of national statistical policies and conducting large-scale sample surveys.
  • Headquartered in New Delhi.
  • Oversees the National Statistical Office (NSO), which compiles key indicators like GDP and IIP.

📌 Critical Minerals and the Energy Transition

  • Includes essential elements such as lithium, cobalt, nickel, and rare earth elements.
  • Vital for manufacturing electric vehicle (EV) batteries, wind turbines, and solar panels.
  • India's critical mineral mission aims to secure domestic value chains and reduce import reliance.

📌 Constitutional Provisions on Legislative Power (Article 246)

  • Outlines the division of powers between the Union and States through the Seventh Schedule.
  • Contains the Union List, State List, and Concurrent List.
  • Ensures cooperative federalism while delineating legislative domains.

📌 Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI)

  • Established in 1963 by a resolution of the Ministry of Home Affairs, later transferred to the Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions.
  • Derives its investigative legal authority from the Delhi Special Police Establishment Act, 1946.
  • Headquartered in New Delhi, headed by a Director.

📌 Pumped Storage Plants (PSP) in Hydro Energy

  • Acts as a giant water battery storing electricity by pumping water from a lower reservoir to an upper reservoir during low-demand periods.
  • Releases water to generate electricity via turbines during peak demand hours.
  • Crucial for balancing grid fluctuations caused by intermittent renewable sources like solar and wind.
