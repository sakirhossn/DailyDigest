Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Foreign Exchange Reserves in India

  • Maintained and managed by the Reserve Bank of India under the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
  • Consists of Foreign Currency Assets (FCAs), Gold, SDRs, and IMF Reserve Tranche Position.
  • Crucial economic indicator used to assess external sector stability and country's import cover.

📌 Strait of Hormuz Geopolitical Corridor

  • A vital maritime choke point connecting the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea.
  • Bordered by Oman (Musandam exclave) on the south and Iran on the north.
  • Handles a major portion of global petroleum and liquefied natural gas (LNG) transit shipments.

📌 Rampur Bushahr and Himachal Pradesh Heritage

  • Rampur Bushahr was a historic hill state situated on the banks of the Sutlej River in Himachal Pradesh.
  • Famous for hosting the ancient annual international Lavi Fair, rooted in historic trade ties with Tibet.
  • Capital of Himachal Pradesh: Shimla (Summer), Dharamshala (Winter).

📌 National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) Surveys

  • Established on July 12, 1982, based on the recommendations of the B. Sivaraman Committee.
  • Apex development financial institution in India for agriculture and rural development.
  • Headquarters: Mumbai, Maharashtra; responsible for periodic rural financial and consumption pattern surveys.

📌 Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA)

  • Governs corporate affairs in India primarily through the Companies Act, 2013 and Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008.
  • Responsible for administering laws relating to companies, LLPs, and professional bodies like ICAI, ICSI, and ICAI(cost).
  • Plays a key role in monitoring youth skilling and internship frameworks like the PM Internship Scheme.
