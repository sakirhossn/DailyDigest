Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 National Disaster Response Fund (NDRF) Architecture

  • Established under Section 46 of the Disaster Management Act, 2005, managed by the Central Government for meeting expenses during emergency relief, rescue, and rehabilitation.
  • Placed in the Public Account of India under 'reserve funds not bearing interest'; funds do not require an annual parliamentary appropriation lapse at year-end.
  • Audited annually by the Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) of India, supplementing State Disaster Response Funds (SDRF) when calamities exceed state budgetary capacity.

📌 Kerch Strait & Black Sea Maritime Geography

  • Connects the Sea of Azov in the north to the Black Sea in the south; flanked by Crimea's Kerch Peninsula and Russia's Taman Peninsula.
  • Spanned by the 19 km Kerch Bridge (Crimean Bridge), the longest bridge built by the Russian Federation, serving both road and railway connectivity.
  • The Sea of Azov, bordered by Ukraine and Russia, is universally acknowledged as the shallowest inland sea in the world with a maximum depth of approximately 14 metres.

📌 Seismic Hazard Zoning of India (IS 1893:2016)

  • The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) categorizes the Indian landmass into four seismic zones: Zone II (Low), Zone III (Moderate), Zone IV (Severe), and Zone V (Very Severe). Note that Zone I was eliminated in 2002.
  • Zone V accounts for the highest risk zone (Zone Factor 0.36) and encompasses the entire North-East India, parts of Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand (including Chamoli), Rann of Kutch (Gujarat), and the Andaman & Nicobar Islands.
  • The Himalayan seismic hazard is driven by the active north-northeast convergence of the Indian tectonic plate against the Eurasian plate at a rate of 40–50 mm per year.

📌 RBI Regulatory Framework for Digital Lending (2022)

  • Mandates that loan disbursals and repayments must execute strictly between bank accounts of the borrower and the regulated entity (RE) without pass-through via third-party escrow pools.
  • Requires regulated entities to provide borrowers with a standardized Key Fact Statement (KFS) disclosing the all-inclusive Annual Percentage Rate (APR), recovery mechanisms, and cooling-off periods.
  • Prohibits predatory practices such as accessing mobile phone resources (biometrics, contacts, media files) without explicit consent, curbing data exploitation.

📌 SAMADHAN Doctrine on Left-Wing Extremism (LWE)

  • Introduced by the Ministry of Home Affairs in 2017 to provide an integrated operational framework to counter Maoist insurgency.
  • Stands for: S - Smart Leadership, A - Aggressive Strategy, M - Motivation and Training, A - Actionable Intelligence, D - Dashboard Based KPIs, H - Harnessing Technology, A - Action plan for each theatre, N - No access to financing.
  • Complemented by the Security Related Expenditure (SRE) scheme and Special Infrastructure Scheme (SIS) to strengthen police stations across affected districts.
