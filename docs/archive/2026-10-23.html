Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Index of Eight Core Industries (ICI)

  • Published monthly by the Office of Economic Adviser, Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
  • Represents 40.27% of the weight of items included in the Index of Industrial Production (IIP) with base year 2011-12.
  • Petroleum Refinery Products carries the highest weight (28.04%), while Fertilizer holds the lowest weight (2.63%) among the eight sectors.

📌 Tenth Schedule of the Indian Constitution

  • Inserted by the 52nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1985 to curb political opportunism and defection.
  • The 91st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003 omitted the provision protecting defections involving one-third splits, requiring at least two-thirds of members to validate a merger.
  • In the landmark Kihoto Hollohan case (1992), the Supreme Court ruled that the Presiding Officer's decision on disqualification is subject to judicial review.

📌 United Nations Security Council (UNSC) Architecture

  • Formed in 1945 under the United Nations Charter, the Council comprises 15 members: 5 permanent (P5) with veto power and 10 non-permanent members.
  • Non-permanent members are elected by the UN General Assembly for two-year terms based on equitable regional distribution.
  • Under Article 25 of the UN Charter, all member states agree to accept and carry out the binding decisions of the Security Council.

📌 Strait of Hormuz: Geographic and Strategic Significance

  • A maritime chokepoint situated between the Musandam peninsula (Oman/UAE) and Iran, connecting the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman.
  • It provides the only sea passage from the Persian Gulf to the open ocean, handling nearly 20-21 million barrels of petroleum liquids daily.
  • Recognized under the UN Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS) as a vital international transit strait subject to the regime of transit passage.

📌 National Organ and Tissue Transplant Organisation (NOTTO)

  • Set up under the Directorate General of Health Services, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, headquartered in New Delhi.
  • Serves as the apex national centre for coordination, networking, procurement, and distribution of organs and tissues across India.
  • Governed under the framework of the Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act (THOTA), 1994, which officially recognized brain-stem death.
