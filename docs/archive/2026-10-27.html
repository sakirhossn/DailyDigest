Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management (FRBM) Act, 2003

  • Enacted to introduce legal transparency and institutionalize fiscal discipline in India's public finances.
  • Originally targeted the elimination of revenue deficit and reduction of fiscal deficit to 3% of GDP.
  • Frequently amended to accommodate economic shocks and extraordinary public expenditure cycles.

📌 Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) Institutional Framework

  • Launched in April 2017 by the National Statistical Office (NSO) under MoSPI.
  • Designed to measure employment and unemployment indicators in both rural and urban areas on an annual and quarterly basis.
  • Utilizes Usual Principal and Subsidiary Status (UPSS) and Current Weekly Status (CWS) approaches to capture workforce dynamics.

📌 Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)

  • A central sector financing facility launched in 2020 under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.
  • Provides medium-to-long term debt financing facility for investment in viable projects for post-harvest management infrastructure.
  • Offers interest subvention of 3% per annum up to a loan amount of ₹2 crore.

📌 IndusInd Bank: Establishment and Headquarters

  • Founded in April 1994 by then Union Finance Minister Manmohan Singh as one of the new-generation private sector banks.
  • Headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra, with its registered office in Mumbai.
  • Named after the Indus Valley civilization, reflecting its core founding philosophy.

📌 Free On Board (FOB) vs. Landed Cost in Trade

  • FOB means the seller is responsible for the goods until they are loaded onto the shipping vessel at the port of origin.
  • Landed cost incorporates the total cost of goods including shipping, freight insurance, customs duties, and port handling charges.
  • Crucial concept for petroleum refiners managing import logistics amidst volatile international shipping freight rates.
