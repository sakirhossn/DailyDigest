Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Article 324 and the Election Commission of India

  • Established under Article 324 of the Constitution, the ECI is a permanent and independent body.
  • Responsible for administering Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha, State Legislative Assembly, and Presidential/Vice-Presidential elections.
  • Chief Election Commissioner and Election Commissioners are appointed by the President with tenure provisions of 6 years or up to 65 years of age.

📌 El Niño and Southern Oscillation (ENSO)

  • Refers to the periodic warming of sea surface temperatures across the central and east-central Equatorial Pacific.
  • Directly impacts global atmospheric circulation, frequently weakening the Indian summer monsoon and causing agricultural droughts.
  • Monitored extensively by global meteorological agencies using indicators like the Southern Oscillation Index (SOI).

📌 Metro Rail Governing Framework in India

  • Metro rail projects are typically implemented through Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs) formed as joint ventures between Central and State governments.
  • Regulated primarily under the Metro Railways (Operation and Maintenance) Act, 2002.
  • Funding models combine equity contributions, bilateral/multilateral international loans, and Transit-Oriented Development (TOD) revenues.

📌 Consumer Price Index (CPI) in India

  • Measures changes in the price level of a weighted average market basket of consumer goods and services purchased by households.
  • Computed and published by the National Statistical Office (NSO) on a monthly basis.
  • Serves as the primary benchmark for the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) in formulating monetary policy and inflation targeting.

📌 India-Nepal Treaty of Peace and Friendship (1950)

  • Signed on July 31, 1950, establishing a close strategic and economic relationship between the two neighbouring sovereign nations.
  • Permits free movement of citizens across the international border without passports or visas.
  • Provides reciprocal treatment to Indian and Nepalese citizens in economic, residential, and employment privileges.
