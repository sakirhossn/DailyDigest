Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Foreign Exchange Reserves of India

  • Managed by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) under the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, and Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999.
  • Consists of four components: Foreign Currency Assets (FCA), Gold, Special Drawing Rights (SDRs), and Reserve Tranche Position (RTP) in the IMF.
  • Foreign Currency Assets (FCA) form the largest portion and are expressed in terms of US dollars, accounting for exchange rate fluctuations of non-US currencies.

📌 Strait of Hormuz

  • A narrow maritime passage located between the Persian Gulf and the Gulf of Oman, separating Iran to the north from Oman and the UAE to the south.
  • Recognized as the world's most critical oil chokepoint, through which approximately 20% to 30% of global petroleum production flows daily.
  • Crucial for India's energy security, as over 60% of India's crude oil imports originate from West Asian suppliers using this route.

📌 Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)

  • Constituted under Section 45ZB of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, following the 2016 statutory amendment.
  • Comprises 6 members: the RBI Governor (Ex-officio Chairperson), Deputy Governor in charge of monetary policy, one RBI officer, and 3 external members appointed by the Central Government.
  • Operates under a Flexible Inflation Targeting framework, mandated to maintain Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation at 4% with an allowable tolerance band of +/- 2%.

📌 Gross Domestic Product (GDP) Measurement in India

  • Real GDP measures total economic output at constant base year prices to eliminate inflation effects, whereas Nominal GDP measures output at current market prices.
  • The current official base year for GDP calculation in India is 2011-12, maintained by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI).
  • Gross Value Added (GVA) reflects sector-wise supply dynamics; GDP is derived as GVA plus product taxes minus product subsidies.

📌 Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) System

  • Launched by the Government of India on 1st January 2013 to transfer welfare benefits directly into bank accounts, eliminating intermediaries and leakage.
  • Powered by the 'JAM Trinity'—Jan Dhan bank accounts, Aadhaar unique identity, and Mobile connectivity.
  • Managed by the DBT Mission, which functions under the Cabinet Secretariat to coordinate implementation across central and state ministries.
