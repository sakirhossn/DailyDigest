Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Directorate General of Trade Remedies (DGTR)

  • Established as an integrated single-window agency under the Department of Commerce in 2018 (merging DGAD and DG Safeguards).
  • Headquartered in New Delhi, it conducts investigations under the Customs Tariff Act, 1975, and WTO agreements.
  • Recommends anti-dumping duties, countervailing measures (subsidies), and safeguard duties; final duty imposition is notified by the Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance.

📌 Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS) Scheme

  • Introduced in April 2010 by the Department of Fertilizers, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers.
  • Governs non-urea fertilizers containing Nitrogen (N), Phosphate (P), Potash (K), and Sulphur (S), with annual/biannual subsidy rates fixed per kilogram of nutrient.
  • Aims to promote balanced fertilizer application, improve soil health, and limit excessive reliance on urea.

📌 Foreign Currency Non-Resident (Bank) [FCNR(B)] Accounts

  • Permits Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs) to maintain term deposits in designated foreign currencies (e.g., USD, EUR, GBP, JPY).
  • The exchange risk is borne by the accepting bank, shielding the depositor from rupee fluctuation risks.
  • Tenure ranges from 1 year to 5 years, and interest income earned in these accounts is exempt from Indian income tax.

📌 Special Drawing Rights (SDRs)

  • Created by the International Monetary Fund (IMF) in 1969 as a supplementary international reserve asset (often termed 'paper gold').
  • Value is based on a basket of five major currencies: US Dollar, Euro, Chinese Renminbi, Japanese Yen, and British Pound.
  • Forms an integral component of India's official foreign exchange reserves alongside foreign currency assets, gold, and reserve tranche.

📌 Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)

  • Constituted as a statutory body under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, under the administrative purview of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.
  • Headquartered in New Delhi, it sets science-based standards for food articles and regulates manufacture, storage, distribution, and sale.
  • Empowers designated Food Safety Officers and state commissioners to issue improvement notices, suspension orders, and product recalls for non-compliant establishments.
