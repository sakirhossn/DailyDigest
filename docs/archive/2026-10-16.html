Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 International Monetary Fund (IMF)

  • Created in 1944 at the Bretton Woods Conference, operational in December 1945.
  • Headquartered in Washington, D.C., United States.
  • Publishes flagship reports including the World Economic Outlook (WEO) and Global Financial Stability Report.

📌 World Bank Group

  • Established in 1944 alongside the IMF at the Bretton Woods Conference.
  • Comprises five distinct institutions: IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, and ICSID.
  • Focuses on long-term capital investment for poverty reduction and economic development.

📌 Election Commission of India (ECI)

  • An autonomous constitutional authority responsible for administering election processes in India under Article 324.
  • Originally a single-member body, it became a multi-member body with the Election Commissioner Amendment Act, 1989.
  • Superintends, directs, and controls elections to Parliament, State Legislatures, and the offices of the President and Vice-President.

📌 Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

  • India's central banking institution established on April 1, 1935, based on the recommendations of the Hilton Young Commission.
  • Nationalized on January 1, 1949.
  • Acts as the banker to the government, regulator of commercial banks, and custodian of the country's foreign exchange reserves.

📌 Wholesale Price Index (WPI)

  • Measures and tracks the changes in the price of goods in the wholesale stage before they are sold retail.
  • Published monthly by the Office of the Economic Adviser, Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
  • Serves as a key indicator to measure headline inflation pressures in producer markets.
