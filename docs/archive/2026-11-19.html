Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Archaeological Survey of India (ASI)

  • Founded in 1861 by Alexander Cunningham during the British Raj.
  • Functions as an attached office under the Ministry of Culture, Government of India.
  • Responsible for archaeological research and the protection of cultural monuments of national importance under the Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Sites and Remains Act, 1958.

📌 India Meteorological Department (IMD)

  • Established in 1875, functioning under the Ministry of Earth Sciences, Government of India.
  • Headquarters: New Delhi.
  • Principal agency responsible for meteorological observations, weather forecasting, and seismology in India.

📌 National Informatics Centre (NIC)

  • Established in 1976 under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).
  • Provides technology-driven solutions to central and state governments, powering key digital portals like VAHAN and digital governance infrastructure.
  • Headquarters: New Delhi.

📌 Jal Jeevan Mission

  • Launched in 2019, implemented by the Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation under the Ministry of Jal Shakti.
  • Aims to provide functional household tap connections (FHTC) to every rural household in India.
  • Operates on a community-led approach with extensive focus on water quality surveillance.

📌 Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP)

  • Enshrined in Part IV (Articles 36–51) of the Indian Constitution, borrowed from the Irish Constitution.
  • Non-justiciable in nature, serving as fundamental guidelines for framing laws and governance policies.
  • Aims to establish a social and economic democracy in the country.
