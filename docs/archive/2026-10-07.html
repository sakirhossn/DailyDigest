Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Article 279A of the Indian Constitution

  • Provides for the establishment of the Goods and Services Tax (GST) Council.
  • Empowers the President to constitute the Council comprising the Union Finance Minister and State Ministers.
  • Decisions at the Council require a weighted majority of not less than three-fourths of the votes cast.

📌 World Bank Group Institutions

  • Comprises five distinct international organizations: IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, and ICSID.
  • Established alongside the International Monetary Fund at the 1944 Bretton Woods Conference.
  • Headquartered in Washington, D.C., United States.

📌 Repo Rate and Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)

  • Repo rate is the interest rate at which the RBI lends money to commercial banks against government securities.
  • The MPC was established under Section 45ZB of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
  • It consists of six members: three from RBI and three appointed by the Central Government.

📌 Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI)

  • Functions as the nodal agency for planned statistical activities in India.
  • Responsible for the formulation and publication of key macroeconomic data like GDP and IIP.
  • Oversees the National Statistical Office (NSO).

📌 European Union Artificial Intelligence Act

  • Recognized as the world's first comprehensive legal framework governing artificial intelligence.
  • Adopts a risk-based approach, banning systems with unacceptable risks while strictly regulating high-risk AI applications.
  • Enforces transparency obligations, including watermarking for generative AI content.
