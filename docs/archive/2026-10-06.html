Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Kakatiya Dynasty & Thousand Pillar Temple

  • Historical Era: Ruled eastern Deccan region (Telangana & Andhra Pradesh) from 12th to 14th century AD; famous capital was Orugallu (modern Warangal).
  • Architecture: Developed unique 'Trikutalayam' triple-shrine style; constructed Thousand Pillar Temple (1163 AD) and Ramappa Temple (UNESCO World Heritage Site).
  • Key Ruler: King Rudra Deva I erected the Rudreswara temple dedicated to Shiva, Vishnu, and Surya.

📌 MSME Classification Framework in India

  • Governing Act: Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (MSMED) Act, 2006.
  • Composite Criteria: Based on both Investment in Plant & Machinery/Equipment AND Annual Turnover.
  • Classification Thresholds: Micro (<₹1 Cr Inv / <₹5 Cr Turnover), Small (<₹10 Cr Inv / <₹50 Cr Turnover), Medium (<₹50 Cr Inv / <₹250 Cr Turnover).

📌 Election Commission of India (ECI) & Article 324

  • Constitutional Status: Permanent constitutional body vested with powers under Article 324 for superintendence, direction, and control of elections.
  • Composition: Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) and two Election Commissioners; decisions taken by majority.
  • Appointment: Appointed by the President of India on recommendation of a selection committee under the CEC and Other ECs Act, 2023.

📌 Goods and Services Tax (GST) Council

  • Constitutional Provision: Inserted via Article 279A under the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016.
  • Chairperson: Union Finance Minister serves as the ex-officio Chairperson.
  • Voting Structure: Centre holds 1/3rd voting weightage; all States combined hold 2/3rd weightage. Decisions require a 3/4th (75%) weighted majority.

📌 Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (PNGRB)

  • Statutory Mandate: Formed under the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board Act, 2006.
  • Primary Function: Regulates refining, transportation, distribution, storage, and sale of petroleum products and natural gas.
  • City Gas Distribution (CGD): Authorizes entities to build and operate local pipeline networks for PNG and CNG across geographical areas.
