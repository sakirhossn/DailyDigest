Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)

  • Launched in: 2020 as part of the Atmanirbhar Bharat package.
  • Financing mechanism: Provides medium-long term debt financing facility for investment in viable projects for post-harvest management infrastructure.
  • Nodal Ministry: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.

📌 74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992

  • Constitutional status: Added Part IX-A to the Constitution, titled 'The Municipalities'.
  • New Schedule: Introduced the Twelfth Schedule containing 18 functional items for urban local bodies.
  • Mandatory provisions: Regular elections every five years, constitution of State Finance Commissions.

📌 Tropical Cyclone Classification by IMD

  • Wind speed parameters: Deep Depression ranges from 51 to 61 kmph, while Cyclonic Storm ranges from 62 to 88 kmph.
  • Basin jurisdiction: North Indian Ocean covers the Bay of Bengal and the Arabian Sea.
  • Naming convention: Managed by WMO/ESCAP panel consisting of 13 member countries.

📌 Tunnel Boring Machine (TBM) Technology

  • Function: A machine used to excavate tunnels with a circular cross section through a variety of soil and rock strata.
  • Advantage: Limits ground disruption and constructs a smooth tunnel wall, reducing lining costs.
  • Strategic push: India is increasingly developing indigenous manufacturing facilities for TBMs to support complex rail and hydro projects.

📌 State Finance Commission (SFC)

  • Constitutional basis: Formed under Article 243-I (for Panchayats) and Article 243-Y (for Municipalities).
  • Appointment: Constituted every five years by the Governor of the State.
  • Core mandate: Reviews the financial position of local bodies and recommends principles for tax devolution.
