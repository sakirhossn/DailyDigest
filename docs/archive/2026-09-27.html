Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005 & Section 24

  • Enacted in 2005 under Article 19(1)(a) implied fundamental rights to freedom of speech and expression.
  • Section 24 grants explicit exemption from RTI disclosures to specific central and state security/intelligence organizations.
  • Proviso to Section 24 mandates that information regarding corruption and human rights violations must be disclosed within 45 days upon approval from the Information Commission.

📌 V. O. Chidambaranar Port (Thoothukudi)

  • Located in Tamil Nadu, it is one of the 12 major ports under the administrative control of the Union Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways.
  • It is an artificial deepwater port situated on the Gulf of Mannar, named after freedom fighter V. O. Chidambaram Pillai.
  • Functions as a major hub for coal, containerized cargo, liquid cargo, and fertilizer trade along South India's eastern seaboard.

📌 Manual for Drought Management in India

  • Published by the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare to standardize scientific criteria for declaring drought.
  • Droughts are categorized into Hydro-meteorological, Agricultural, and Socio-economic droughts based on specific indices.
  • Requires mandatory assessment of rainfall indices, dry spells, Reservoir Storage Index (RSI), and Crop Conditions.

📌 World Trade Organization (WTO) Tariffs & GATT

  • Established on January 1, 1995, following the Marrakesh Agreement, replacing the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT).
  • Headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, governing international trade rules among 164 member states.
  • Operates on the Most-Favoured-Nation (MFN) principle, ensuring non-discriminatory treatment between trading partners.

📌 Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023

  • Provides a legal framework for processing digital personal data in India balancing individual rights with lawful data processing needs.
  • Introduces roles such as 'Data Principal' (the individual) and 'Data Fiduciary' (entity determining processing purpose).
  • Establishes the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) to adjudicate non-compliance and impose penalties up to ₹250 crore per breach.
