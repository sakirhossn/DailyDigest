Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) Framework

  • Launched by the National Sample Survey Office (NSSO) under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) in 2017.
  • Generates quarterly key labour force indicators (Labour Force Participation Rate, Worker-Population Ratio, Unemployment Rate) for urban areas using Current Weekly Status (CWS).
  • Produces annual reports covering both rural and urban sectors using Usual Status (ps+ss) and Current Weekly Status (CWS).

📌 Constitutional Safeguards of the Election Commission (Article 324)

  • Established on 25 January 1950 (commemorated annually as National Voters' Day); initially a single-member body before becoming multi-member in October 1993.
  • Article 324(5) establishes that the Chief Election Commissioner cannot be removed except in like manner and on the like grounds as a Judge of the Supreme Court of India.
  • Other Election Commissioners cannot be removed from office except on the recommendation of the Chief Election Commissioner.

📌 Kharai Camels of Kutch

  • Recognised as the ninth indigenous camel breed of India by the National Bureau of Animal Genetic Resources (NBAGR) in 2015.
  • Famous as 'swimming camels' endemic to the arid coastal regions of Kutch, Gujarat, adapted to swim long distances across tidal creeks.
  • Feed predominantly on coastal mangroves (Avicennia marina), making them ecologically reliant on conserved intertidal marine zones.

📌 IMD Tropical Cyclone Classification System

  • India Meteorological Department (IMD) acts as the Regional Specialised Meteorological Centre (RSMC) for the North Indian Ocean basin (Bay of Bengal and Arabian Sea).
  • Classifications by wind speed: Depression (31-49 km/h), Deep Depression (50-61 km/h), Cyclonic Storm (62-87 km/h), Severe Cyclonic Storm (88-117 km/h), and Very Severe Cyclonic Storm (118-166 km/h).
  • Systems exceeding 222 km/h (120 knots or more) are designated as Super Cyclonic Storms.

📌 Ethanol Blended Petrol (EBP) Programme

  • Administered by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas (MoPNG) to reduce oil import dependency, lower carbon emissions, and support sugarcane/grain farmers.
  • Permits procurement of ethanol from multiple feedstocks: sugarcane juice, B-heavy molasses, C-heavy molasses, damaged food grains, surplus FCI rice, and maize.
  • India achieved 10% ethanol blending in petrol in June 2022 and moved forward the target for achieving 20% blending (E20) to ESY 2025-26.
