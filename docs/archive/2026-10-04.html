Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (PNGRB)

  • Established under the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board Act, 2006.
  • Headquartered in New Delhi; functions under the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas.
  • Regulates downstream activities including refining, processing, storage, transportation, distribution, and sale of petroleum products and natural gas.

📌 Prevention of Corruption Act (PCA), 1988

  • Enacted to consolidate laws relating to the prevention of corruption and bribery among public servants.
  • Section 17A (inserted via 2018 amendment) mandates prior approval from the government/appointing authority before conducting any inquiry or investigation against a public official.
  • Special Judges appointed under Section 3 of the Act exercise exclusive jurisdiction over trial of offences specified under the PCA.

📌 Coal India Limited (CIL)

  • Formed in 1975 following the nationalization of coal mines; holds Maharatna status.
  • Headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal.
  • Operates through eight fully owned subsidiaries across major Indian coalfields and contributes over 80% of domestic coal production.

📌 Universal Service Obligation (USO) in Retail Fuel Marketing

  • Governed by the revised guidelines for authorization to market transportation fuels notified in November 2019.
  • Requires authorized entities to set up a minimum of 5% of their total retail outlets in specified remote areas.
  • Mandates uninterrupted supply of specified auto fuels at all authorized retail outlets.

📌 Office of the United States Trade Representative (USTR)

  • Created as the Office of the Special Trade Representative under the Trade Expansion Act of 1962.
  • Part of the Executive Office of the President of the United States.
  • Leads U.S. participation in multilateral trade bodies such as the World Trade Organization (WTO) and negotiates bilateral trade pacts.
