Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Cauvery River System & Interstate River Water Disputes Act

  • Originates at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri range of the Western Ghats (Kodagu district, Karnataka) and empties into the Bay of Bengal at Poompuhar, Tamil Nadu.
  • Major tributaries include Harangi, Hemavati, Shimsha, Arkavathi (left bank) and Lakshmantirtha, Kabini, Bhavani, Noyyal, Amaravati (right bank).
  • Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 1956 was enacted under Article 262; it excludes Supreme Court jurisdiction over tribunal awards once finalized under the Act.

📌 Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB)

  • Established on 17 March 1986 under the provisions of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985.
  • Headquartered in New Delhi, it functions as the national nodal agency under the Ministry of Home Affairs for coordinating anti-narcotics enforcement.
  • Implements India's obligations under the UN Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs (1961) and UN Convention Against Illicit Traffic in Narcotic Drugs (1988).

📌 Supreme Court Central Empowered Committee (CEC)

  • Originally constituted by the Supreme Court in May 2002 to monitor compliance with court orders on forestry, wildlife, and environmental conservation.
  • Restructured by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) into a permanent statutory oversight body under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 in 2023.
  • Comprises a chairperson, member secretary, and three expert members with expertise in forestry and environment.

📌 National Bank for Financing Infrastructure and Development (NaBFID)

  • Set up under the National Bank for Financing Infrastructure and Development Act, 2021 as a specialized Development Financial Institution (DFI).
  • Regulated and supervised by the Reserve Bank of India as an All India Financial Institution (AIFI), alongside NABARD, NHB, EXIM Bank, and SIDBI.
  • Headquartered in Mumbai, it focuses on long-term non-recourse debt financing for infrastructure projects listed in the National Infrastructure Pipeline (NIP).

📌 Sapinda Relationship in Indian Family Law

  • Defined under Section 3(f) of the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955: extends up to the third generation in the line of ascent through the mother, and the fifth generation through the father.
  • Section 5(v) of the Act stipulates that parties must not be 'sapindas' of each other unless a recognized custom permits such union.
  • A marriage solemnized in violation of sapinda rules without custom is null and void ab initio under Section 11 of the Act.
