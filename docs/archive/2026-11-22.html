Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Index of Eight Core Industries in India

  • Comprises 8 core sectors: Coal, Crude Oil, Natural Gas, Refinery Products, Fertilizers, Steel, Cement, and Electricity.
  • Calculated and published monthly by the Office of the Economic Adviser, Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
  • Has a combined weight of 40.27% in the Index of Industrial Production (IIP).

📌 Constitutional Provisions for High Court Judges

  • Judges of High Courts are appointed by the President of India under Article 217.
  • The retirement age for High Court judges is 62 years (unlike Supreme Court judges who retire at 65).
  • Transfer of High Court judges is carried out by the President after consultation with the Chief Justice of India under Article 222.

📌 Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries (OPEC)

  • Established in Baghdad, Iraq, in September 1960 by founding members Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, and Venezuela.
  • Headquartered in Vienna, Austria, since 1965.
  • Aims to coordinate and unify petroleum policies among member countries to secure fair and stable prices for petroleum producers.

📌 State Election Commission in India

  • Constitutional body created under Article 243K of the Indian Constitution.
  • Responsible for superintending, directing, and controlling the preparation of electoral rolls and conducting elections to Panchayats and Municipalities.
  • The State Election Commissioner is appointed by the Governor of the State.

📌 Commercial Coal Mining and Auctions in India

  • Commercial mining allows private entities to mine coal for commercial sale rather than captive consumption alone.
  • Regulated under the Coal Mines (Special Provisions) Act and mines are auctioned transparently via online platforms.
  • Aimed at reducing import dependency and accelerating domestic coal production.
