Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Goods and Services Tax (GST) Council

  • Constitutional status under Article 279A, added by the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016.
  • Chaired by the Union Finance Minister, with Union Minister of State for Finance and State Finance Ministers as members.
  • Decision-making requires a 3/4th weighted majority; Centre holds 1/3rd voting power, States collectively hold 2/3rd.

📌 Bharat Stage (BS) Emission Standards

  • Modeled on European emission standards (Euro norms) and regulated by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB).
  • India skipped BS-V and directly adopted BS-VI nationwide on April 1, 2020.
  • BS-VII norms incorporate Real Driving Emissions (RDE) alongside limits on brake wear and microplastic tyre wear.

📌 Skill India Mission & PM-SETU Scheme

  • PM-SETU operates under the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE).
  • Focuses on modernising Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs) via Industry-Academia Cluster Partnerships.
  • Aims to align technical and vocational education directly with Industry 4.0 job demands.

📌 Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve & Derivatives Framework

  • Regulated under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999 by the Reserve Bank of India.
  • Mandates capital/reserve cushions on currency derivative exposures above specific thresholds (e.g., $2 million).
  • Aims to curb systemic risks arising from unhedged corporate foreign currency liabilities.

📌 Non-Tariff Barriers (NTBs) under WTO Framework

  • Includes administrative rules, SPS (Sanitary and Phytosanitary) measures, and technical barriers to trade (TBT).
  • Regulated under the WTO Agreement on Technical Barriers to Trade to prevent trade distortion.
  • Unlike customs tariffs, NTBs restrict market access through complex compliance regulations rather than direct import duties.
