Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Supreme Court Original Jurisdiction (Article 131)

  • Enshrined under Article 131 of the Indian Constitution.
  • Grants the Supreme Court exclusive authority to adjudicate disputes between the Government of India and one or more States.
  • Does not extend to disputes arising out of a treaty, covenant, or agreement entered into before the commencement of the Constitution.

📌 National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD)

  • Established on 12 July 1982 based on the recommendations of the B. Sivaraman Committee.
  • Headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra.
  • Operates as an apex regulatory body for overall licensing and supervision of Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) and Cooperative Banks in India.

📌 Strait of Hormuz Geopolitical Significance

  • A crucial maritime choke point located between Oman and Iran.
  • Connects the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea.
  • Vital for global energy transit, carrying a significant percentage of the world's petroleum petroleum liquids.

📌 Constitutional Provision on Concurrent List (Schedule VII)

  • The Seventh Schedule of the Constitution of India contains three legislative lists: Union List, State List, and Concurrent List.
  • The Concurrent List includes subjects on which both Parliament and State Legislatures can make laws (e.g., Education, Forests).
  • Adopted from the Constitution of Australia.

📌 Bond Yields and Interest Rate Dynamics

  • Bond yield represents the return an investor realizes on a bond.
  • There is an inverse relationship between bond prices and bond yields in secondary debt markets.
  • Central bank interest rate hikes typically push secondary bond yields higher, impacting commercial bank treasury portfolios.
