Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Bank for International Settlements (BIS)

  • Established in May 1930, the BIS is the world's oldest international financial organization.
  • Headquartered in Basel, Switzerland, with representative offices in Hong Kong SAR and Mexico City.
  • Owned by 63 member central banks representing countries that together account for about 95% of world GDP.

📌 Central Warehousing Corporation (CWC)

  • Established in 1957 under the Agricultural Produce (Development and Warehousing) Corporations Act, 1956.
  • Functions as a Category-I Mini-Ratna Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution.
  • Provides logistics support, scientific storage, and warehousing facilities for agricultural produce and commercial goods across India.

📌 Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)

  • Statutory body established under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006.
  • Operates under the administrative control of the Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.
  • Responsible for laying down science-based standards for articles of food and regulating their manufacture, storage, distribution, and sale.

📌 India Meteorological Department (IMD)

  • Established in 1875 in response to a tropical cyclone that hit Kolkata in 1864 and subsequent famines.
  • Headquartered in New Delhi (initially headquartered in Kolkata, then Shimla and Pune).
  • Functions under the Ministry of Earth Sciences and is one of the six Regional Specialized Meteorological Centres of the World Meteorological Organization (WMO).

📌 BRICS & New Development Bank (NDB)

  • The term 'BRIC' was coined in 2001 by Jim O'Neill; the grouping officially held its first summit in 2009 in Yekaterinburg, Russia.
  • South Africa joined in December 2010, turning it into BRICS; five new members (Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, Saudi Arabia [pending formalization], UAE) were invited in 2024.
  • The New Development Bank (NDB) was established by BRICS nations during the 6th BRICS Summit in Fortaleza (2014) with an initial capital of $100 billion.
