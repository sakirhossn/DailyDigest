Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Index of Industrial Production (IIP)

  • Tracks manufacturing, mining, and electricity production sectors in India.
  • Released monthly by the National Statistical Office (NSO), MoSPI.
  • Core industries hold a weight of over 40% within the IIP index basket.

📌 Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA)

  • A deep free trade pact covering goods, services, investment, and economic cooperation.
  • India-UAE CEPA marked a historic bilateral trade milestone signed in 2022.
  • Differs from a standard FTA by featuring expansive institutional regulatory commitments.

📌 Open Market Operations (OMOs)

  • Quantitative monetary policy tool deployed by the RBI.
  • Involves direct sale and purchase of government securities to regulate liquidity.
  • Helps stabilize bond yields and control money supply in the domestic market.

📌 Registration of Electors Rules, 1960

  • Formulated under the Representation of the People Act, 1950.
  • Govern electoral roll preparation, voter registration, objections, and deletions.
  • Form-7 is specifically designated for raising objections or requesting name removals.

📌 Gandhi Memorial Museum, Madurai

  • Located in the historic Tamukkam Palace of Madurai, Tamil Nadu.
  • One of five national Gandhi Sangrahalayas established nationwide.
  • Preserves important memorabilia, photographs, and historical letters associated with Mahatma Gandhi.
