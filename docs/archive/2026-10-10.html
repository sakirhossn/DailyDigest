Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Airports Authority of India (AAI)

  • Established on 1st April 1995 by merging National Airports Authority and International Airports Authority of India under AAI Act, 1994.
  • Headquartered at Rajiv Gandhi Bhawan, Safdarjung Airport, New Delhi; operates under Ministry of Civil Aviation.
  • Manages 137 airports, including 24 International airports, 10 Custom airports, and 80 Domestic airports.

📌 Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024

  • Enacted to curb paper leaks, impersonation, and fraudulent practices in public recruitment exams.
  • Prescribes minimum punishment of 3 to 5 years imprisonment, extendable to 10 years for organized crime syndicates.
  • Imposes minimum fines of ₹1 crore on organized entities involved in compromising examination security.

📌 Article 293 of the Indian Constitution

  • Deals with financial borrowing powers of Indian States within and outside their territorial limits.
  • Clause (3) restricts a state from raising loans without Central Government consent if previous central loans remain unpaid.
  • The Centre fixes Net Borrowing Ceilings (NBC) annually based on recommended Fiscal Deficit limits (3% of GSDP under FRBM).

📌 PM-eBus Sewa Scheme

  • Launched by Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) to deploy 10,000 electric buses in urban centers.
  • Covers urban areas having population between 3 lakh and 40 lakh as per 2011 census.
  • Implemented on Gross Cost Contract (GCC) Public-Private Partnership model with Central Assistance.

📌 Electoral Roll Revisions under RP Act, 1950

  • Section 21 of the Representation of the People Act, 1950 governs preparation and revision of electoral rolls.
  • Qualifying dates for voter registration are January 1, April 1, July 1, and October 1 under Election Laws (Amendment) Act, 2021.
  • Special Intensive Revision (SIR) involves door-to-door verification by Block Level Officers (BLOs) appointed under Article 324.
