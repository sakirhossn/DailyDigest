Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)

  • Statutory body constituted under Section 45ZB of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
  • Comprises 6 members: RBI Governor (Chairperson), RBI Deputy Governor in charge of monetary policy, one officer of RBI, and three external members appointed by the Central Government.
  • Mandated to maintain price stability while keeping in mind the objective of growth, targeting inflation at 4% with a +/- 2% tolerance band.

📌 Goods and Services Tax (GST) Council

  • Constitutional body established under Article 279A of the Indian Constitution, introduced via the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016.
  • Chaired by the Union Finance Minister, with State Finance Ministers or representatives acting as members.
  • Functions on the principle of cooperative federalism, requiring a three-fourths majority of weighted votes for decision-making.

📌 Foreign Exchange Reserves of India

  • Managed by the Reserve Bank of India under the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
  • Comprise Foreign Currency Assets (FCAs), Gold, Special Drawing Rights (SDRs), and Reserve Tranche Position with the International Monetary Fund (IMF).
  • Acts as a critical financial buffer against external shocks and maintains orderly conditions in the foreign exchange market.

📌 BRICS Bloc Evolution

  • Originally formed as BRIC in 2009 by Brazil, Russia, India, and China; South Africa joined in 2010 to form BRICS.
  • Headquarters of its financial arm, the New Development Bank (NDB), is located in Shanghai, China.
  • Focuses on strengthening economic cooperation, alternative international payment platforms, and multilateral trade initiatives among developing economies.

📌 Central Secretariat Library (CSL)

  • One of the oldest government libraries in India, tracing its origins back to the late 19th century (founded around 1891 in Calcutta before moving to Delhi).
  • Functioning under the administrative control of the Ministry of Culture, Government of India.
  • Serves as an invaluable repository of rare official documents, reports, and gazettes for researchers and policymakers.
