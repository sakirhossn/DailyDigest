Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 State Election Commission (Article 243K)

  • Constitutional status: Inserted via the 73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendment Acts, 1992 under Articles 243K and 243ZA.
  • Appointment & Removal: Appointed by the Governor; can be removed from office only in like manner and on like grounds as a Judge of a High Court.
  • Jurisdiction: Vested with the superintendence, direction, and control of the preparation of electoral rolls and conduct of all elections to Panchayats and Municipalities.

📌 Ways and Means Advances (WMA) Scheme

  • Statutory basis: Governed under Section 17(5) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
  • Purpose: Temporary loan facilities extended by the RBI to Central and State governments to bridge transient mismatches in cash receipts and disbursements.
  • Operational tenures: Advances must be repaid within 90 days from the date of advance, with interest charged at the prevailing repo rate.

📌 Representation of the People Act, 1950 (RPA 1950)

  • Scope: Provides for the allocation of seats in the House of the People and State Legislative Assemblies/Councils.
  • Voter Roll Framework: Details the qualifications of voters and the statutory machinery for preparing and revising electoral rolls.
  • Contrast with RPA 1951: RPA 1950 handles seats and registration of electors, while RPA 1951 governs the actual conduct of elections, qualifications/disqualifications of candidates, and corrupt practices.

📌 National Disaster Response Force (NDRF)

  • Legal Mandate: Constituted under Section 44 of the Disaster Management Act, 2005 as a specialized force for disaster response.
  • Administrative Control: Operates under the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), led by a Director General (DG) drawn from the Indian Police Service (IPS).
  • Motto & Composition: Its motto is 'Aapda Seva Sadaiv Sarvatra'; personnel are drawn on deputation from Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) like BSF, CRPF, CISF, and ITBP.

📌 Securities Transaction Tax (STT)

  • Introduction: Enacted under Chapter VII of the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2004, spearheaded by the Union Ministry of Finance.
  • Nature: A direct tax levied on every purchase or sale of equities, equity-oriented mutual funds, and derivative contracts listed on recognized Indian stock exchanges.
  • Purpose: Designed to curb tax evasion on capital gains and establish a transparent, automated transaction trail for financial securities.
