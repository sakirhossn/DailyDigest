Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 New Zealand Capital and Currency

  • Capital City: Wellington
  • Currency: New Zealand Dollar (NZD)
  • Geographical Note: Island country in the southwestern Pacific Ocean comprising the North Island and South Island.

📌 Panchayati Raj System & Constitutional Provisions

  • Constitutional Amendment: 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992
  • Part IX of the Constitution spanning Articles 243 to 243-O
  • Mandates a three-tier system of panchayats at village, intermediate, and district levels.

📌 RoDTEP Scheme Framework

  • Full Form: Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products
  • Objective: To refund central, state, and local duties/taxes incurred on exported goods that were hitherto un-refunded.
  • WTO Compliance: Designed as a fully WTO-compliant mechanism replacing older export incentive programs.

📌 Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016

  • Replaced the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995.
  • Increased benchmark disabilities from 7 to 21 categories, incorporating acid attack victims and speech-language disabilities.
  • Ensures statutory reservations in higher education and government employment.

📌 India-United States 2+2 Ministerial Dialogue

  • Participants: Ministers of External Affairs and Defense from India alongside US Secretaries of State and Defense.
  • Serves as the highest-level institutional forum for strategic, geopolitical, and security cooperation.
  • Focuses on maritime security, defense technology sharing, and intelligence coordination.
