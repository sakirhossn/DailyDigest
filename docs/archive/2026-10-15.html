Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 International Monetary Fund (IMF) and Governance Quotas

  • Established in 1944 at the Bretton Woods Conference, operational in December 1945.
  • Headquartered in Washington, D.C., United States.
  • Governed by 190 member countries, with voting power determined by quota subscriptions reflecting relative economic position in the world economy.

📌 Wholesale Price Index (WPI) in India

  • Measures the changes in the prices of goods sold and traded in wholesale markets.
  • Published monthly by the Office of Economic Adviser in the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
  • Does not capture services inflation, which is a key distinction from the Consumer Price Index (CPI).

📌 Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI)

  • Prime investigative agency of India operating under the Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions.
  • Derived its legal mandate from the Delhi Special Police Establishment (DSPE) Act, 1946.
  • First established through a resolution of the Ministry of Home Affairs in April 1963.

📌 Securities and Emergency Regulations on Telecom Infrastructure

  • Under the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885, temporary suspension of telecom services is governed by specific oversight rules notified in 2017.
  • Requires orders to be issued by competent authorities such as the Union Home Secretary or State Home Secretaries during public emergencies.
  • Subject to judicial review by Constitutional Courts to ensure proportionality and protection of fundamental rights.

📌 Hosur Industrial Cluster (Tamil Nadu)

  • Strategically located near the border of Tamil Nadu and Karnataka, functioning as a major manufacturing hub.
  • Hosts prominent automotive, electrical machinery, and expanding aerospace and defence manufacturing units.
  • Plays an instrumental role in Tamil Nadu's industrial export strategy and regional economic development.
