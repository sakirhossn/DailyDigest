Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO)

  • Established under the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952; functions under the Ministry of Labour and Employment.
  • Administered by the Central Board of Trustees (CBT), a tripartite body comprising representatives from Government, Employers, and Employees.
  • Headquartered in New Delhi; manages three major schemes: EPF Scheme 1952, EPS (Pension Scheme) 1995, and EDLI (Insurance Scheme) 1976.

📌 Foreign Currency Non-Resident (Bank) Deposit Scheme - FCNR(B)

  • Introduced to allow Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) to maintain term deposits in foreign currencies in India.
  • Deposits are fully repatriable (both principal and interest), and exchange rate risk is entirely borne by the commercial bank, not the depositor.
  • Exempt from Indian income tax and wealth tax, serving as an important source of foreign exchange inflow during external current account deficits.

📌 Meenakshi Amman Temple & Dravidian Architecture

  • Located in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, on the southern banks of the Vaigai River.
  • Renowned for its towering Gopurams (monumental entrance towers), golden lotus tank (Porthamarai Kulam), and Hall of Thousand Pillars.
  • Key structural expansions were carried out during the reign of the Nayak Dynasty (notably King Thirumalai Nayak) in the 16th–17th centuries.

📌 Outer Space Treaty of 1967

  • Formal title: Treaty on Principles Governing the Activities of States in the Exploration and Use of Outer Space, including the Moon and Other Celestial Bodies.
  • Forms the foundational framework of international space law under the auspices of the United Nations Committee on the Peaceful Uses of Outer Space (COPUOS).
  • Explicitly bans placing weapons of mass destruction (WMDs) in orbit, on the Moon, or on any other celestial body, reserving space for peaceful activities.

📌 Operation Polo & Article 371(J) (Kalyana-Karnataka)

  • Operation Polo was the military action conducted in September 1948 by the Indian Armed Forces to integrate the princely state of Hyderabad into the Union of India.
  • September 17 is celebrated annually as Liberation Day across the region (Kalyana-Karnataka).
  • Article 371(J) was added to the Constitution via the 98th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2012, to provide special developmental provisions and reservation quotas for six backward districts of the region.
