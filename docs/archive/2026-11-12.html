Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013

  • Enacted on September 12, 2013, to provide food and nutritional security by ensuring access to adequate quantity of quality food at affordable prices.
  • Covers up to 75% of rural and 50% of urban population under Targeted Public Distribution System (TPDS).
  • Entitles beneficiaries under Priority Households to 5 kg of foodgrains per person per month at subsidized prices of ₹3/kg for rice, ₹2/kg for wheat, and ₹1/kg for coarse grains.

📌 Assisted Reproductive Technology (Regulation) Act, 2021

  • Provides for the regulation and supervision of ART clinics and ART banks to prevent misuse and ensure ethical practice.
  • Establishes National Board and State Boards to lay down code of conduct and minimum standards of equipment and manpower.
  • Sets upper age limits for ART procedures: 50 years for women and 55 years for men.

📌 Rabi Cropping Season in India

  • Sowing starts in winter from October to December, and harvesting takes place in spring from April to June.
  • Major Rabi crops include wheat, barley, gram, peas, mustard, and rapeseed.
  • Rabi crops depend significantly on sub-soil moisture and winter rainfall brought by Western Disturbances.

📌 Confederation of Indian Industry (CII)

  • Non-governmental, non-profit, industry-led and industry-managed organization founded in 1895.
  • Headquartered in New Delhi, it plays a proactive role in India's development process through policy advocacy.
  • Partners with state and central governments to organize flagship summits like the Partnership Summit.

📌 Credit Rating Agencies Regulation in India

  • Credit Rating Agencies (CRAs) assess the creditworthiness of corporate and government debt securities.
  • Regulated primarily by SEBI under the SEBI (Credit Rating Agencies) Regulations, 1999.
  • Major CRAs registered in India include CRISIL, ICRA, CARE, and India Ratings and Research.
