Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Central Water Commission (CWC)

  • Established in 1945 on the advice of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar as the Central Waterways, Irrigation and Navigation Commission (CWINC).
  • Apex technical organization under the Ministry of Jal Shakti, functioning as an attached office headquartered in New Delhi.
  • Entrusted with initiating, coordinating, and managing schemes for water resource control, conservation, and utilization for flood mitigation, irrigation, and hydropower.

📌 Fertilizer Sector Regulatory Architecture in India

  • Fertilizers are classified as Essential Commodities under the Essential Commodities Act (ECA), 1955, administered by the Department of Fertilizers.
  • Urea operates under statutory price control, whereas Phosphatic and Potassic (P&K) fertilizers fall under the decontrolled Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS) regime initiated in 2010.
  • 100% of indigenous and imported agricultural urea is coated with neem oil to retard nitrogen release and prevent industrial diversion.

📌 Major Port Authorities Act, 2021

  • Replaced the Major Port Trusts Act, 1963, granting enhanced operational autonomy to 11 of the 12 Major Ports in India.
  • Abolished the Tariff Authority for Major Ports (TAMP), allowing port management boards to set tariff guidelines based on market dynamics.
  • India contains 12 Major Ports governed centrally, alongside approximately 200 notified Non-Major Ports governed by State Maritime Boards under the concurrent list.

📌 Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999

  • Replaced the stringent Foreign Exchange Regulation Act (FERA), 1973, shifting the paradigm from 'regulation and criminalization' to 'management and civil facilitation'.
  • Classifies international transactions into Current Account (generally unrestricted) and Capital Account (regulated under specific RBI guidelines).
  • The Enforcement Directorate (ED) serves as the investigative agency responsible for enforcing FEMA compliance and penalizing civil infractions.

📌 Konkan Railway Corporation Limited (KRCL)

  • Constructed along the rugged Sahyadri mountain range between Roha (Maharashtra) and Thokur (Karnataka), spanning 741 kilometers.
  • Incorporated as a public sector undertaking in 1990 under pioneer civil engineer E. Sreedharan, featuring the iconic Karbude Tunnel and Panval Nadi viaduct.
  • Operates as a multi-state joint venture between the Ministry of Railways and the state governments of Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka, and Kerala.
