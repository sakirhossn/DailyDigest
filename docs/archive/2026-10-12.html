Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Strait of Hormuz & Maritime Navigation

  • Geographical Location: Connects the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea.
  • Strategic Importance: Key global oil transit choke point through which approximately 20% of global petroleum passes.
  • Bordering Nations: Iran to the north and north-east, Oman and the United Arab Emirates to the south.

📌 Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) Framework

  • Definition: Fee charged to merchants for processing payments through debit/credit cards or digital wallets.
  • Zero-MDR Policy: Mandated by the Government of India for BHIM-UPI and RuPay debit card transactions under Section 10A of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007.
  • Stakeholder Burden: Costs of processing zero-MDR transactions are currently subsidized by government budget allocations to banks.

📌 Non-Performing Asset (NPA) Classification Rules

  • Standard Definition: A loan asset becomes non-performing when interest or principal remains overdue for more than 90 days.
  • Sub-categories: NPAs are categorized into Substandard (NPA for <= 12 months), Doubtful (NPA for > 12 months), and Loss Assets.
  • Special Mention Accounts (SMA): SMA-0 (1-30 days overdue), SMA-1 (31-60 days overdue), and SMA-2 (61-90 days overdue) serve as early warning signals.

📌 Fundamental Right to Freedom of Assembly

  • Constitutional Provision: Article 19(1)(b) grants all Indian citizens the right to assemble peaceably and without arms.
  • Reasonable Restrictions: Article 19(3) allows Parliament to impose restrictions based on 'sovereignty and integrity of India' or 'public order'.
  • Statutory Power: Section 144 of the Code of Criminal Procedure / Section 163 of Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita allows magistrates to issue temporary orders in urgent cases of nuisance or danger.

📌 Decriminalization Provisions under CGST Act

  • Section 132 Framework: Deals with offences including tax evasion, fake invoicing, and wrongful input tax credit utilization.
  • Prosecution Threshold: Raised from ₹2 crore to ₹5 crore for general offences to prevent unwarranted criminal proceedings.
  • Compounding of Offences: Allows offenders to pay a compounding fee to avoid court prosecution under Section 138 of the CGST Act.
