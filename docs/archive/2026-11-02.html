Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Goods and Services Tax (GST) Council (Article 279A)

  • Constitutional Body established under Article 279A following the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016.
  • Chaired by the Union Finance Minister, with Union Minister of State for Finance and state Finance Ministers as members.
  • Decisions are taken by a 75% weighted majority; the Central Government holds one-third (33.33%) voting weight, while all States collectively hold two-thirds (66.67%).

📌 Special Additional Excise Duty (SAED) / Windfall Profit Tax

  • Introduced by India on July 1, 2022, on crude oil production and exports of petrol, diesel, and ATF under the Central Excise Act.
  • Aimed at absorbing supernormal profits earned by domestic refineries profiting from high international refining margins ('crack spreads').
  • The rates are reviewed on a fortnightly basis depending on the trailing two-week averages of international benchmark fuel prices.

📌 IMD Classification of Cyclonic Disturbances

  • Deep Depression: 28–33 knots (52–61 km/h); Cyclonic Storm: 34–47 knots (62–88 km/h).
  • Severe Cyclonic Storm (SCS): 48–63 knots (89–117 km/h); Very Severe Cyclonic Storm (VSCS): 64–89 knots (118–166 km/h).
  • Extremely Severe Cyclonic Storm (ESCS): 90–119 knots (167–221 km/h); Super Cyclonic Storm (SuCS): ≥120 knots (≥222 km/h).

📌 Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology (SCTIMST)

  • Located in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala; declared an Institute of National Importance under the Department of Science and Technology (DST) via an Act of Parliament in 1980.
  • Specializes in advanced biomedical engineering, biomaterials development, and specialized neuro-cardiac clinical research.
  • Pioneered the development of India's first indigenous artificial heart valve ('Chitra Valve') and blood bag systems.

📌 Shey Phoksundo National Park & Dolpa District

  • Largest national park in Nepal, covering 3,555 sq km in the Trans-Himalayan region across Dolpa and Mugu districts.
  • Houses the famous oligotrophic Phoksundo Lake (declared a Ramsar site in 2007) and preserves endangered Himalayan fauna including the snow leopard and blue sheep.
  • Dolpa is physically the largest district of Nepal, bordered by Tibet (China) to the north.

Under Article 279A of the Indian Constitution, what voting weight is allocated to the Central Government in decisions taken by the GST Council?

  • A) One-fourth (25%)
  • B) One-third (33.33%)
  • C) One-half (50%)
  • D) Two-thirds (66.67%)
Click to reveal Answer & Explanation
Correct Answer: Option B
Explanation: According to Article 279A(9), every decision of the GST Council is taken by a majority of not less than three-fourths (75%) of the weighted votes of members present and voting. The vote of the Central Government has a weight of one-third of total votes cast, while all State Governments combined carry a weight of two-thirds.
