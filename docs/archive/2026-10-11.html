Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)

  • Established in 2008 as an umbrella organization for operating retail payments and settlement systems in India.
  • Promoted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and the Indian Banks' Association (IBA) under the provisions of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007.
  • Headquarters: Mumbai, Maharashtra; responsible for foundational systems like UPI, IMPS, RuPay, and Aadhaar Enabled Payment System (AePS).

📌 Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999

  • Enacted by the Parliament of India to replace the restrictive Foreign Exchange Regulation Act (FERA).
  • Aims to facilitate external trade and payments and promote the orderly development and maintenance of the foreign exchange market in India.
  • Administered by the Enforcement Directorate (under the Ministry of Finance) for investigation of violations, while RBI handles regulatory policy framing.

📌 Udyam Registration Portal for MSMEs

  • Launched by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises for online registration of MSMEs based on Aadhaar verification.
  • Classification criteria: Micro (Investment up to ₹1 crore, Turnover up to ₹5 crore); Small (Investment up to ₹10 crore, Turnover up to ₹50 crore); Medium (Investment up to ₹50 crore, Turnover up to ₹250 crore).
  • Registration is entirely paperless and free of cost.

📌 Article 355 of the Indian Constitution

  • Part of Emergency Provisions (Part XVIII) of the Constitution of India.
  • Mandates that the Union Government shall protect every State against external aggression and internal disturbance.
  • Ensures that the government of every State is carried on in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution.

📌 Repo Rate and Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF)

  • Repo rate is the interest rate at which the Reserve Bank of India lends money to commercial banks against government securities.
  • Operates under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) window managed by the central bank.
  • Serves as the primary benchmark rate used by the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) to control domestic inflation and liquidity.
