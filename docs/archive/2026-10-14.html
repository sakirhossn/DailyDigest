Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries (OPEC)

  • Established in Baghdad in September 1960 by five founding members: Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, and Venezuela.
  • Headquartered in Vienna, Austria since 1965.
  • Controls a substantial portion of global crude oil production and heavily influences international petroleum pricing.

📌 World Economic Outlook (WEO)

  • A flagship survey published twice a year by the International Monetary Fund (IMF).
  • Analyzes global economic developments during the near and medium term.
  • Provides critical macro-projections on global GDP growth, inflation, and trade balances.

📌 National Thermal Power Corporation (NTPC)

  • India's largest power utility company, founded in 1975 under the Ministry of Power.
  • Awarded Maharatna status by the Government of India in 2010.
  • Currently spearheading clean energy transitions into solar, wind, and green hydrogen projects.

📌 Biodiversity Heritage Sites and Ecosystem Assessment

  • Ecosystem Red List frameworks evaluate the risk of ecosystem collapse using criteria modeled after IUCN species assessments.
  • Aims to protect functional ecological units rather than isolated endangered flora and fauna.
  • Crucial for biodiversity conservation planning under the Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD).

📌 Indian Railways Freight Operations

  • Freight traffic is the primary revenue earner for Indian Railways, subsidizing passenger travel costs.
  • Key commodities transported include coal, iron ore, cement, food grains, and fertilizers.
  • Dedicated Freight Corridors (DFC) are being constructed to enhance transit speed and logistics capacity.
