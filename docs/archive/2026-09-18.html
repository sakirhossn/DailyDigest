Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Public Accounts Committee (PAC)

  • First set up in 1921 under the provisions of the Government of India Act, 1919; currently consists of 22 members (15 Lok Sabha, 7 Rajya Sabha).
  • Members are elected annually from among Parliament members according to the principle of proportional representation by means of a single transferable vote.
  • Examines the annual audit reports submitted by the CAG under Article 151 of the Constitution to ensure administrative accountability.

📌 National Land Monetization Corporation (NLMC)

  • Established in March 2022 as a wholly owned Government of India company under the Ministry of Finance.
  • Initial authorized share capital stands at ₹15,000 crore, with a paid-up share capital of ₹150 crore.
  • Mandated to undertake monetization of surplus, unused, or underused non-core land and building assets of CPSEs and government agencies.

📌 National Quantum Mission (NQM)

  • Approved by the Union Cabinet in April 2023 with an outlay of ₹6,003 crore for the period 2023-31.
  • Nodal Agency: Department of Science & Technology (DST), Ministry of Science and Technology.
  • Focuses on developing intermediate-scale quantum computers (50-1000 physical qubits) and secure Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) systems.

📌 Ethanol Blending Programme (EBP) in India

  • Launched in 2003 by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas (MoPNG) to reduce import dependency and carbon footprint.
  • Targeted 20% ethanol blending in petrol (E20) moved forward from 2030 to 2025-26 under the updated National Policy on Biofuels.
  • Ethanol is an ethyl alcohol ($C_2H_5OH$) produced mainly by fermenting sugar derived from sugarcane molasses, corn, broken rice, and damaged food grains.

📌 Merchant Discount Rate (MDR)

  • MDR is the charge payable by a merchant to the bank accepting card/digital payments to cover transaction handling costs.
  • The fee is typically split among the issuing bank, acquiring bank, and payment network processor (e.g., NPCI, Visa, Mastercard).
  • Zero-MDR policy was mandated by the Government from January 1, 2020, for UPI and RuPay debit card transactions to boost digital adoption.
