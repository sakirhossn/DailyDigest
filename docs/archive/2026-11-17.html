Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Poverty Estimation Methodologies in India (Tendulkar & Rangarajan Panels)

  • Suresh Tendulkar Committee (2009) moved away from calorie-intake norms to uniform consumption baskets across rural/urban areas using Mixed Reference Periods (MRP).
  • C. Rangarajan Committee (2014) recommended poverty thresholds based on daily per-capita spending: ₹32 for rural and ₹47 for urban areas.
  • NITI Aayog now releases the National Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI) based on 12 indicators across Health, Education, and Standard of Living.

📌 Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In)

  • Designated under Section 70B of the Information Technology (IT) Act, 2000 as the national agency for cyber incident response.
  • Operates under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) with headquarters in New Delhi.
  • Cybersecurity directives issued in 2022 mandate critical entities to report specified cyber incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours.

📌 Special Additional Excise Duty (SAED) & Divisible Pool Dynamics

  • SAED is an excise duty surcharge levied on crude oil production and transport fuel exports under the Central Excise Act, 1944.
  • Under Article 270 of the Constitution, revenue collected via specific cesses and surcharges is not mandatorily shared with States unless provided by Parliament.
  • Recommendations of the 15th Finance Commission set the horizontal tax devolution to states at 41% of the central divisible pool.

📌 National Steel Policy (NSP) 2017 & Indian Mineral Resources

  • The National Steel Policy 2017 envisions achieving 300 Million Tonnes (MT) of crude steel capacity and 255 MT of production by 2030-31.
  • India's per capita steel consumption target under NSP 2017 is slated to reach 160 kg by 2030-31.
  • Hematite and Magnetite are the two primary iron ores mined in India; Hematite accounts for roughly 79% of total iron ore geological reserves.

📌 Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPI) Regulatory Architecture

  • FPIs represent foreign entities investing in Indian secondary securities markets without acquiring direct management control.
  • Investments below 10% of a company's paid-up equity capital are categorized as FPI; holdings exceeding 10% are reclassified as Foreign Direct Investment (FDI).
  • Designated Depository Participants (DDPs) registered with SEBI are responsible for scrutinizing and granting registration to incoming FPIs.
