Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Wholesale Price Index (WPI) in India

  • Measures the average change in prices of goods sold in the wholesale market prior to retail distribution.
  • Compiled and published weekly/monthly by the Office of Economic Adviser, Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
  • Does not track services inflation; covers primary articles, manufactured products, and fuel & power.

📌 Contempt of Courts Act, 1971

  • Statute that classifies contempt into civil contempt (willful disobedience of court orders) and criminal contempt.
  • Criminal contempt covers actions that scandalize the court or prejudice judicial proceedings.
  • Supreme Court (Article 129) and High Courts (Article 215) possess inherent constitutional powers to punish for contempt.

📌 ASEAN (Association of Southeast Asian Nations)

  • Founded on August 8, 1967, through the signing of the Bangkok Declaration by five original member nations.
  • Headquarters: Jakarta, Indonesia.
  • Comprises 10 Southeast Asian member states; India is a dialogue partner and active participant in the East Asia Summit.

📌 Windfall Tax in Public Finance

  • A higher tax levied by governments on specific industries or companies when economic circumstances yield sudden, extraordinary profits.
  • Frequently applied to domestic crude oil producers and refiners when international energy prices spike unexpectedly.
  • Aims to capture excess economic rents to fund welfare measures or absorb high consumer energy burdens.

📌 Quad (Quadrilateral Security Dialogue)

  • Strategic security dialogue between Australia, India, Japan, and the United States.
  • Originally conceptualized in 2007 by Japanese PM Shinzo Abe, revived in 2017 for a free and open Indo-Pacific.
  • Focuses on maritime security, humanitarian assistance, disaster relief, and critical emerging technologies.
