Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023

  • Enacted to repeal and replace the colonial Indian Penal Code (IPC), 1860, coming into nationwide effect on 1 July 2024.
  • Contains 358 sections compared to the 511 sections of the erstwhile IPC, consolidating offences against women and children in Chapter V.
  • Introduces community service as an official form of penal punishment for minor offences.

📌 Section 26(2) of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Act, 1934

  • Empowers the Central Government to declare that any series of banknotes of any denomination shall cease to be legal tender, based on the recommendation of the Central Board of the RBI.
  • The Supreme Court of India upheld the constitutional validity of the 2016 demonetisation notification under Section 26(2) by a 4:1 majority in Vivek Narayan Sharma v. Union of India (2023).
  • The sole dissenting opinion was delivered by Justice B.V. Nagarathna, who noted that large-scale demonetisation ought to occur via plenary legislation.

📌 Northeast Monsoon (Retreating Monsoon) in India

  • Occurs predominantly from October to December, driven by high-pressure building over Central Asia and the sub-Himalayan belt.
  • Serves as the primary rainy season for the Coromandel Coast, contributing nearly 50% to 60% of Tamil Nadu's annual precipitation.
  • Winds travel over the Bay of Bengal, picking up moisture before striking the coastal belts of Tamil Nadu, Rayalaseema, and coastal Andhra Pradesh.

📌 Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA)

  • Established by the Government of India under the APEDA Act passed by Parliament in December 1985; headquartered in New Delhi.
  • Functions under the administrative purview of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
  • Entrusted with monitoring imports and promoting exports of scheduled products, including cereals, non-basmati rice, alcoholic beverages, and meat.

📌 Dandakaranya Plateau and Indravati River

  • Dandakaranya is a physiographic plateau region covering parts of Chhattisgarh (Bastar division), Odisha, and Andhra Pradesh.
  • The Indravati River originates in the Eastern Ghats in Kalahandi, Odisha, flows westward through Bastar, and joins the Godavari River at Bhadrakali.
  • Famous for the Chitrakote Falls on the Indravati River, commonly known as the 'Niagara of India' due to its wide horseshoe shape during peak monsoon.
