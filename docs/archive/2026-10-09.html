Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Integrated Goods and Services Tax (IGST)

  • Governed by the IGST Act, 2017, and administered under Article 269A of the Indian Constitution.
  • Collected by the Central Government on all inter-state trade of goods and services as well as on import transactions.
  • Revenue collected under IGST is subsequently apportioned between the Union and the respective destination states.

📌 State Level Bankers' Committee (SLBC)

  • A state-level apex inter-institutional forum established under the lead bank scheme by the Reserve Bank of India.
  • Responsible for reviewing priority sector lending, agricultural credit expansion, and grassroots financial inclusion targets.
  • Chaired by the Chairman, Managing Director, or Executive Director of the designated Lead Bank operating within the state.

📌 Representation of the People Act, 1951

  • Enacted by the Provisional Parliament under Article 327 of the Constitution of India.
  • Contains comprehensive provisions regarding the conduct of parliamentary and state legislative elections, qualification of members, and corrupt practices.
  • Empowers the Election Commission of India to conduct byelections within stipulated statutory timeframes upon a seat falling vacant.

📌 Global Capability Centers (GCCs) in India

  • Corporate offshore units established by multinational companies to handle R&D, IT services, and back-office operations.
  • India hosts a significant share of the world's GCCs, primarily concentrated in tech hubs like Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, and Chennai.
  • Transitioning rapidly from mere cost-arbitrage centers to high-value global innovation and engineering leadership hubs.

📌 Geopolitics of the Persian Gulf and Choke Points

  • Includes vital strategic straits such as the Strait of Hormuz, connecting oil-producing Gulf nations to world markets.
  • Recognized globally as a key energy corridor where maritime safety directly influences international crude oil pricing.
  • Monitored closely by energy security agencies and international naval coalitions to ensure unhindered commercial transit.
