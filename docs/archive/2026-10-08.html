Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Structure

  • Established on April 1, 1935, in accordance with the provisions of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
  • Originally privately owned, the RBI was nationalized in 1949 and is headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra.
  • The central bank is governed by a central board of directors appointed by the Government of India.

📌 World Bank Group Institutions

  • Founded in 1944 at the Bretton Woods Conference, originally focusing on post-war reconstruction.
  • Consists of five distinct international organizations: IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, and ICSID.
  • Headquartered in Washington, D.C., its primary mission is poverty reduction and fostering sustainable economic development.

📌 Goods and Services Tax (GST) Council

  • Constitutional body established under Article 279A of the Indian Constitution introduced by the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016.
  • Chaired by the Union Finance Minister, with state finance ministers serving as members.
  • Responsible for making recommendations on GST rates, exemptions, thresholds, and tax laws.

📌 El Niño and Indian Monsoon Dynamics

  • El Niño is characterized by the warming of sea surface temperatures in the central and eastern tropical Pacific Ocean.
  • It typically disrupts atmospheric circulation patterns, often leading to suppressed rainfall and drought conditions in the Indian subcontinent.
  • Monsoon performance critically dictates agricultural output and rural demand cycles across Indian states.

📌 Gross Value Added (GVA) in National Accounts

  • Measures the value of goods and services produced in an area, industry, or sector of an economy.
  • Calculated as Output minus Intermediate Consumption; it provides sector-specific performance data before accounting for taxes and subsidies.
  • Compiled and published periodically by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) in India.
