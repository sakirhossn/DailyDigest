Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Consumer Price Index (CPI) and Inflation Measurement in India

  • CPI in India is compiled and published monthly by the National Statistical Office (NSO) under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI).
  • The base year for the current CPI series is 2012.
  • The Reserve Bank of India utilizes CPI-Combined as its primary gauge for inflation targeting.

📌 Credit Rating Agencies and Moody's

  • Moody's Investors Service is an American credit rating agency founded in 1909 by John Moody.
  • Along with S&P and Fitch, Moody's forms part of the 'Big Three' credit rating agencies globally.
  • Sovereign ratings assigned by these agencies influence foreign institutional investments and sovereign borrowing costs.

📌 Vande Bharat Express Project

  • Vande Bharat Express is an indigenous, semi-high-speed, self-propelled trainset introduced under the 'Make in India' initiative.
  • The trains are capable of operating at speeds up to 160-180 km/h.
  • They feature advanced passenger amenities including automatic doors, GPS-based passenger information systems, and onboard Wi-Fi.

📌 Escrow Account Mechanism in Finance

  • An escrow account is a temporary financial instrument held by a third-party neutral entity during a transaction or contractual agreement.
  • It safeguards both buyers and sellers by ensuring funds are only released when pre-defined conditions are successfully met.
  • Frequently utilized in real estate, mergers and acquisitions, and infrastructure project financing.

📌 Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) Statutory Framework

  • Constituted under Section 45ZB of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
  • Comprises six members: three officials from the RBI and three external members appointed by the Central Government.
  • Tasked with determining the policy repo rate required to achieve the inflation target.
