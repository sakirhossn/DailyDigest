Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 New Development Bank (NDB)

  • Established: 2014 by the Fortaleza Declaration during the 6th BRICS Summit; officially commenced operations in 2015.
  • Headquarters: Shanghai, China (First President was India's K.V. Kamath).
  • Key Objective: Mobilizes resources for infrastructure and sustainable development projects in BRICS and other emerging market economies.

📌 National Investigation Agency (NIA)

  • Statutory Basis: Enacted under the National Investigation Agency (NIA) Act, 2008 following the 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks.
  • Jurisdiction: Functions as the central counter-terrorism agency under the Ministry of Home Affairs, possessing concurrent extraterritorial jurisdiction via 2019 amendments.
  • Headquarters: New Delhi, with regional branches across major Indian states.

📌 Bab al-Mandab Strait

  • Geography: A strategic maritime choke point connecting the Red Sea (north-west) to the Gulf of Aden and the Arabian Sea (south-east).
  • Borders: Separates Yemen on the Arabian Peninsula from Djibouti and Eritrea in the Horn of Africa.
  • Economic Significance: Serves as a crucial conduit for oil shipments passing from the Persian Gulf through the Suez Canal.

📌 Consumer Price Index (CPI) in India

  • Publishing Authority: Compiled and released monthly by the National Statistics Office (NSO), MoSPI.
  • Base Year: Currently set to 2012=100.
  • Policy Use: The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) of the RBI uses CPI-Combined (CPI-C) as its nominal anchor for inflation targeting.

📌 Unified Payments Interface (UPI) Framework

  • Developer: National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) in April 2016.
  • Underlying System: Built over the Immediate Payment Service (IMPS) infrastructure allowing 24/7/365 real-time interbank funds transfer.
  • Key Feature: Operates using a Virtual Payment Address (VPA), eliminating the need to expose sensitive bank account details.
