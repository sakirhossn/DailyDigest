Daily Static GK Booster

📌 Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Limited (DFCCIL)

  • Founding & Mandate: DFCCIL was registered under the Companies Act 1956 in October 2006 as a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) under the Ministry of Railways to design, construct, and operate Dedicated Freight Corridors.
  • Key Corridors: The Eastern DFC (approx. 1,856 km) connects Sahnewal near Ludhiana (Punjab) to Dankuni (West Bengal), while the Western DFC (approx. 1,504 km) links Dadri (Uttar Pradesh) to Jawaharlal Nehru Port Trust (JNPT, Maharashtra).
  • Operational Parameters: DFC tracks are engineered for 32.5-tonne axle loads and maximum train operating speeds of 100 km/h, dramatically improving freight throughput and reducing national logistics costs.
