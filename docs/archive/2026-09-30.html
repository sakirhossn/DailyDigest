Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Index of Industrial Production (IIP)

  • Measures short-term changes in the volume of production of a basket of industrial products during a given period.
  • Calculated and published monthly by the National Statistical Office (NSO), MoSPI.
  • Core sectors include Electricity, Steel, Refinery Products, Crude Oil, Coal, Cement, Natural Gas, and Fertilizers.

📌 Merchant Discount Rate (MDR)

  • Defined as the cost paid by a merchant to a bank for accepting payments from customers via digital methods.
  • Divided between issuer bank, payment gateway providers, and the acquiring bank.
  • Crucial instrument regulated under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007.

📌 Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA)

  • A deep free trade agreement that covers negotiation of trade in services, investment, and other economic partnership areas.
  • India-UAE CEPA was India's first bilateral trade pact in the MENA region, signed in 2022.
  • Aims to significantly expand bilateral trade and economic integration.

📌 Form 6 under Election Commission Rules

  • Statutory document utilized under the Registration of Electors Rules, 1960.
  • Applied by Indian citizens who are eligible to vote for new registration or inclusion of name in electoral rolls.
  • Managed directly under the administrative jurisdiction of the Election Commission of India.

📌 United Nations General Assembly and Security Council Framework

  • UN consists of six principal organs including the General Assembly, Security Council, and International Court of Justice.
  • Headquartered in New York City, United Nations acts as the primary global deliberative and diplomatic forum.
  • António Guterres is the current Secretary-General of the United Nations.
