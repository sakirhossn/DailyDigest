Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Urban Cooperative Banks (UCBs) in India

  • Referred to loosely as cooperative banks in urban and semi-urban areas, originally registered under Cooperative Societies Acts.
  • Regulated jointly by the Reserve Bank of India (banking regulation) and State/Central Registrars of Cooperative Societies (registration and management).
  • Brought under stricter RBI oversight through amendments to the Banking Regulation Act in 2020.

📌 Goods and Services Tax (GST) Council

  • Constitutional body established under Article 279A of the Indian Constitution.
  • Chaired by the Union Minister of Finance, with state finance ministers as members.
  • Responsible for making recommendations on GST rates, exemptions, thresholds, and administrative rules.

📌 El Nino Southern Oscillation (ENSO)

  • A coupled ocean-atmosphere phenomenon spanning the tropical Pacific Ocean.
  • Consists of El Nino (warm phase) and La Nina (cool phase), with a neutral phase in between.
  • Directly influences monsoon rainfall distribution and agricultural output across the Indian subcontinent.

📌 Special Additional Excise Duty (SAED)

  • Also widely known as a windfall tax, levied by governments on unexpected profits accrued by domestic crude oil producers and fuel exporters.
  • Revised fortnightly in India based on international crude oil price fluctuations and domestic refinery margins.
  • Aimed at ensuring domestic availability of petroleum products at reasonable prices.

📌 United Nations General Assembly (UNGA)

  • The primary deliberative, policymaking, and representative organ of the United Nations.
  • Comprises all 193 member states of the United Nations, maintaining equal voting rights (one member, one vote).
  • Headquartered in New York City, USA; passes resolutions that carry significant international moral and legal weight.
