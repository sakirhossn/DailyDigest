Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Goods and Services Tax (GST) Council

  • Constitutional Basis: Article 279A introduced by the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016.
  • Composition: Headed by the Union Finance Minister, with State Finance/Taxation Ministers as members.
  • Voting Mechanism: The Centre has one-third weightage, and the States combined have two-thirds weightage in decision making.

📌 Purchasing Managers' Index (PMI)

  • Definition: An economic indicator derived from monthly surveys of supply chain managers across manufacturing and services.
  • Threshold: A reading above 50 indicates expansion in economic activity compared to the previous month, while below 50 indicates contraction.
  • Publisher: Compiled globally by S&P Global; in India, manufacturing and services PMI are tracked closely as high-frequency indicators.

📌 Strait of Hormuz

  • Geography: A narrow and strategically vital strait located between the Persian Gulf and the Gulf of Oman.
  • Bordering Nations: Iran to the north, and the United Arab Emirates and Oman (Musandam exclave) to the south.
  • Global Significance: Handles approximately one-fifth of the world's total petroleum liquid consumption, making it a critical maritime chokepoint.

📌 Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs)

  • Background: Created as 'Primitive Tribal Groups' in 1975 by the Ministry of Home Affairs and renamed PVTGs in 2006.
  • Criteria: Declared based on a pre-agricultural level of technology, declining or stagnant population, extremely low literacy, and subsistence economy.
  • Total Count: There are 75 notified PVTGs spread across 18 States and one Union Territory (Odisha has the highest number).

📌 Small Savings Schemes in India

  • Management: Administered by the National Savings Institute under the Ministry of Finance (Department of Economic Affairs).
  • Fund Utilization: Collections form part of the National Small Savings Fund (NSSF), which is used to finance central and state fiscal deficits.
  • Revision: Interest rates are notified on a quarterly basis by the Union Ministry of Finance based on formula-based yields of G-secs.
