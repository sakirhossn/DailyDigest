Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Essential Commodities Act, 1955

  • Enacted to control the production, supply, and distribution of essential commodities to maintain fair prices.
  • Administered by the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution.
  • Allows central and state governments to fix stock limits during periods of abnormal price spikes.

📌 Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act - MMDR Act

  • The primary legislation governing the mining sector in India, enacted in 1957.
  • Amended recently to introduce private sector participation in deep-seated critical mineral exploration.
  • Regulates the granting of mining leases and prospecting licenses across states.

📌 Air Quality Index (AQI) in India

  • Launched in 2014 under the Swachh Bharat Abhiyan by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change.
  • Tracks eight major pollutants including PM2.5, PM10, Nitrogen Dioxide, Sulfur Dioxide, Carbon Monoxide, Ozone, Ammonia, and Lead.
  • Calculated and monitored primarily by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB).

📌 SATAT Initiative for Compressed Biogas

  • Stands for 'Sustainable Alternative Towards Affordable Transportation', launched in October 2018.
  • Aimed at setting up Compressed Biogas (CBG) production plants and making it available for market use in automotive fuels.
  • Implemented by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas in partnership with public sector oil marketing companies.

📌 National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)

  • An umbrella organization for operating retail payments and settlement systems in India.
  • Established in 2008 under the provisions of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007.
  • Promoted by the Reserve Bank of India and the Indian Banks' Association (IBA).
