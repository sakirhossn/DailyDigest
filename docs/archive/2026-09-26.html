Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Blue Bonds & Sustainable Finance

  • First issued internationally by the Seychelles in 2018 to support sustainable marine and fisheries projects.
  • Regulated domestically in India under sustainability frameworks recognized by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
  • Primarily designed to fund ocean conservation, renewable energy, and sustainable maritime resource management.

📌 Merchant Discount Rate (MDR)

  • MDR is a fee charged to a merchant by a bank for processing customer payments made via digital channels.
  • It is typically distributed among three entities: the merchant's bank, the customer's bank, and the payment network provider.
  • Zero-MDR policies on UPI have driven massive volume growth, prompting ongoing government deliberations over bank compensation subsidies.

📌 Red Sea & Maritime Chokepoints

  • The Red Sea links the Mediterranean Sea via the Suez Canal to the Indian Ocean via the Bab-el-Mandeb Strait.
  • It handles a significant percentage of global maritime freight and container shipping traffic.
  • Geopolitical instability in the region directly impacts international supply chains, crude oil transit, and commercial shipping insurance costs.

📌 Free Trade Agreements (FTAs)

  • An FTA is an international treaty between two or more economies designed to reduce or eliminate import tariffs and non-tariff trade barriers.
  • Under WTO rules, FTAs fall under regional trade agreements that promote deeper economic integration than standard Most-Favoured-Nation (MFN) tariffs.
  • India's recent FTA engagements focus on comprehensive economic partnership agreements (CEPA) covering trade in goods, services, and investments.

📌 History of Kabaddi at the Asian Games

  • Kabaddi made its formal debut as a medal event at the 1990 Asian Games held in Beijing, China.
  • India has historically dominated both men's and women's kabaddi competitions at the continental level.
  • The sport is governed internationally by the International Kabaddi Federation (IKF), headquartered in India.
