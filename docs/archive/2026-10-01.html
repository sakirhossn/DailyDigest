Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Index of Industrial Production (IIP)

  • Measures the short-term changes in the volume of production of industrial products in an economy.
  • Compiled and published monthly by the National Statistical Office (NSO), MoSPI.
  • Uses eight core industries (Coal, Crude Oil, Natural Gas, Refinery Products, Fertilizers, Steel, Cement, Electricity) which comprise over 40% of the weight of items in IIP.

📌 Strategic Petroleum Reserves (SPR)

  • Emergency stockpiles of crude oil maintained by countries to safeguard against supply disruptions.
  • In India, underground crude oil storage facilities are built at Visakhapatnam, Mangaluru, and Padur.
  • Managed by Indian Strategic Petroleum Reserves Limited (ISPRL), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Oil Industry Development Board (OIDB).

📌 Fiscal Deficit and FRBM Act

  • Represents the total borrowing needs of the government during a fiscal year.
  • Regulated by the Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management (FRBM) Act, enacted in 2003.
  • Aims to introduce fiscal discipline, reduce fiscal deficit, and eliminate revenue deficit over stipulated timelines.

📌 Representation of the People Act, 1951

  • An act of Parliament of India to provide for the conduct of elections to the Houses of Parliament and State Legislatures.
  • Deals with qualifications and disqualifications for membership of those Houses, corrupt practices, and election petitions.
  • Administered by the Election Commission of India under Article 324 of the Constitution.

📌 Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA)

  • A comprehensive free trade agreement that covers negotiation on trade in goods, services, investment, and economic partnership.
  • India-UAE CEPA marked a milestone in bilateral economic diplomacy, boosting market access and investment flows.
  • Encourages institutional cooperation across strategic infrastructure, energy, and digital sectors.
