Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)

  • Established in 1961, succeeding the Organization for European Economic Co-operation (OEEC).
  • Headquarters: Paris, France.
  • Comprises 38 member countries committed to market democracies and free-market economies; India is a key economic partner but not a full member.

📌 Eighth Schedule of the Indian Constitution

  • Contains the officially recognized languages of the Republic of India.
  • Originally included 14 languages, and currently lists 22 languages after successive constitutional amendments.
  • Kannada, the language of the recent ESA draft translation, is one of the classical and constitutionally recognized languages in this schedule.

📌 Free Trade Agreements (FTAs) & Economic Partnerships

  • FTAs involve international treaties that reduce or eliminate tariff and non-tariff barriers between participating nations.
  • Governed internationally by World Trade Organization (WTO) rules under GATT Article XXIV.
  • India actively negotiates Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreements (CEPAs) and FTAs to boost export diversification and secure critical supply chains.

📌 Asian Games and Olympic Council of Asia (OCA)

  • The Asian Games is a continental multi-sport event regulated by the Olympic Council of Asia (OCA).
  • First held in 1951 in New Delhi, India.
  • The 20th Asian Games (2026) are scheduled in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan.

📌 Critical Minerals and Strategic Industry Supply Chains

  • Critical minerals include lithium, cobalt, nickel, and rare earth elements vital for green tech and advanced electronics.
  • India launched missions such as the National Critical Mineral Mission to secure domestic processing and recycling ecosystems.
  • The Ministry of Mines oversees mineral exploration, policy formulation, and structural reform in the sector.
