Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)

  • Established in 1961 with its headquarters located in Paris, France.
  • An international economic organization consisting of 38 member countries aimed at stimulating economic progress and world trade.
  • Publishes key global economic reports including the Economic Outlook and PISA educational assessments.

📌 Article 324 of the Indian Constitution

  • Vests the superintendence, direction, and control of the preparation of electoral rolls and the conduct of elections in the Election Commission.
  • Provides for the composition of the Election Commission consisting of the Chief Election Commissioner and other such number of Commissioners as the President may fix.
  • Guarantees administrative independence to the poll body under constitutional safeguards.

📌 Central Institute of Brackishwater Aquaculture (CIBA)

  • A premier research institute functioning under the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR), Government of India.
  • Headquartered in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, focusing on sustainable brackishwater aquaculture development.
  • Specializes in research on fish and shrimp breeding, aquatic animal health management, and seafood processing technologies.

📌 Free Trade Agreement (FTA) & Preferential Trade

  • An international treaty between two or more economies to reduce or eliminate import tariffs and non-tariff trade barriers.
  • Classified under the WTO framework as Regional Trade Agreements (RTAs), encompassing goods, services, and investment norms.
  • India-New Zealand FTA marks a crucial step in expanding bilateral economic integration across the Indo-Pacific region.

📌 Asian Games (Aichi-Nagoya 2026)

  • The 20th edition of the multi-sport Asian Games hosted in Aichi Prefecture and Nagoya city, Japan.
  • Governed by the Olympic Council of Asia (OCA); held quadrennially as the largest multi-sport event after the Olympic Games.
  • Features diverse sporting disciplines including shooting, badminton, athletics, and traditional regional sports.
