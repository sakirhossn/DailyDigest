Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 New Development Bank (NDB)

  • Established in 2014 via the 6th BRICS Summit in Fortaleza, Brazil; formally operationalized in 2015.
  • Headquartered in Shanghai, China, with an initial authorized capital of $100 billion.
  • Created to mobilize resources for infrastructure and sustainable development projects in BRICS and other emerging economies.

📌 India's Foreign Exchange Reserves

  • Managed and custodian held by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) under the RBI Act, 1934.
  • Comprises 4 assets: Foreign Currency Assets (FCA), Gold, Special Drawing Rights (SDRs), and Reserve Tranche Position (RTP) with IMF.
  • FCA is the largest component and is expressed in US Dollar terms, reflecting valuation changes of non-USD currencies.

📌 Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd (DFCCIL)

  • Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) under the administrative control of the Ministry of Railways.
  • Incorporated in October 2006 under the Companies Act, 1956.
  • Responsible for planning, construction, maintenance, and operation of Eastern (EDFC) and Western (WDFC) Freight Corridors.

📌 International Monetary Fund (IMF)

  • Formed in 1944 at the Bretton Woods Conference; commenced operations in March 1947.
  • Headquartered in Washington, D.C., USA; currently has 190 member countries.
  • Key flagship publications include the World Economic Outlook (WEO) and Global Financial Stability Report.

📌 United Nations Security Council (UNSC)

  • Established under Article 23 of the UN Charter in 1945 as one of six principal UN organs.
  • Composed of 5 Permanent Members with veto power (P5) and 10 Non-Permanent Members elected for 2-year terms by the General Assembly.
  • Headquartered at UN Headquarters in New York City.
