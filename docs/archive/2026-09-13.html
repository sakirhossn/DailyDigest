Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India (DFCCIL)

  • Established in October 2006 under the Companies Act 1956 as a Special Purpose Vehicle owned by the Ministry of Railways.
  • Responsible for planning, construction, maintenance, and operation of two major corridors: Eastern DFC (Ludhiana to Dankuni) and Western DFC (Dadri to JNPT).
  • Headquartered in New Delhi.

📌 National Commission for Protection of Child Rights (NCPCR)

  • Statutory body constituted in March 2007 under the Commission for Protection of Child Rights (CPCR) Act, 2005.
  • Operates under the administrative control of the Ministry of Women and Child Development.
  • Ensures all laws, policies, and administrative mechanisms align with child rights perspectives as per the Indian Constitution and UN Convention on the Rights of the Child.

📌 BRICS & New Development Bank (NDB)

  • BRIC term was coined in 2001 by Jim O'Neill; first formal summit held in Yekaterinburg, Russia in 2009.
  • New Development Bank (NDB) was set up during the 6th BRICS Summit in Fortaleza (2014) with equal voting rights among founding members.
  • NDB is headquartered in Shanghai, China.

📌 National Statistical Office (NSO)

  • Formed by merging the Central Statistics Office (CSO) and National Sample Survey Office (NSSO) under MoSPI.
  • Responsible for publishing key national indicators including Gross Domestic Product (GDP), Index of Industrial Production (IIP), and Consumer Price Index (CPI).
  • Current base year for GDP calculation in India is 2011-12.

📌 Asian Cricket Council (ACC)

  • Established in 1983 as the Asian Cricket Conference to foster and develop cricket across the continent.
  • Headquartered in Colombo, Sri Lanka.
  • Organizes the biennial Asian Cup tournament for both Men's and Women's national cricket teams.
