Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 New Development Bank (NDB)

  • Established by BRICS nations via the Fortaleza Declaration at the 6th BRICS Summit in 2014.
  • Headquartered in Shanghai, China; operates with an initial authorized capital of $100 billion.
  • Aims to mobilize resources for infrastructure and sustainable development projects in BRICS and emerging economies.

📌 Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Limited (DFCCIL)

  • Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) established on 30 October 2006 under the Ministry of Railways.
  • Responsible for planning, construction, operation, and maintenance of Eastern (1,856 km) and Western (1,504 km) DFCs.
  • Headquartered in New Delhi; designed to shift freight traffic from high-density rail tracks to dedicated routes.

📌 Foreign Exchange Reserves Management in India

  • Legal framework provided by Section 17 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
  • Comprises Foreign Currency Assets (FCAs), Gold, SDRs (Special Drawing Rights), and Reserve Tranche Position in IMF.
  • FCA constitutes the largest component and is invested in foreign treasury bills, sovereign bonds, and bank deposits.

📌 Rural Self Employment Training Institutes (RSETIs)

  • Initiative driven jointly by the Ministry of Rural Development (MoRD) and Sponsor/Lead Banks in every district.
  • Modeled on RUDSETI, an initiative originally pioneered in 1982 by SDM Educational Trust, Syndicate Bank, and Canara Bank in Karnataka.
  • Mandated to provide free skill development training to rural youths (aged 18–45) to promote self-employment.

📌 Bail Provisions under Indian Criminal Law

  • Bailable and Non-Bailable offenses defined under Section 2(a) of CrPC (now BNSS 2023 equivalent).
  • Anticipatory bail granted under Section 438 of CrPC (BNSS Section 482) before arrest takes place.
  • Landmark judgment Gurbaksh Singh Sibbia v. State of Punjab (1980) established standard principles governing anticipatory bail.
