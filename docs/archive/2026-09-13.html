Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Kasturirangan Committee on Western Ghats

  • Constituted in 2012 as a High-Level Working Group (HLWG) to review the earlier Madhav Gadgil Commission report on the Western Ghats.
  • Recommended designating approximately 37% (around 60,000 sq km) of the Western Ghats as Ecologically Sensitive Area (ESA).
  • Categorized activities into prohibited (mining, thermal power plants, heavy construction) and regulated categories across six states.

📌 Rural Self Employment Training Institutes (RSETIs)

  • Institutional framework established jointly by the Ministry of Rural Development (MoRD), State Governments, and Lead Banks.
  • Aims to provide short-term skill development training and facilitate credit linkage for rural youth to establish micro-enterprises.
  • Operates under the umbrella of Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM).

📌 Chabahar Port & Strategic Significance

  • Located in southeastern Iran in the Gulf of Oman; comprises two main ports: Shahid Kalantari and Shahid Beheshti.
  • India signed a long-term main contract with Iran's Ports and Maritime Organisation (PMO) through India Ports Global Limited (IPGL).
  • Serves as the key oceanic port facilitating direct trade bypass around Pakistan into Afghanistan and Central Asia.

📌 International Monetary Fund (IMF)

  • Established in 1944 at the Bretton Woods Conference; headquartered in Washington, D.C., USA.
  • Primary objective is ensuring global monetary stability, facilitating international trade, and providing financial assistance via SDR quotas.
  • Publishes flagship reports including the World Economic Outlook (WEO) and Global Financial Stability Report (GFSR).

📌 Freedom of Peaceful Assembly (Article 19(1)(b))

  • Guarantees all citizens the fundamental right to assemble peaceably and without arms under Part III of the Constitution.
  • Subject to reasonable restrictions under Article 19(3) on grounds of the sovereignty and integrity of India, or public order.
  • State authorities enforce regulatory measures (e.g., Section 144 of CrPC / Section 163 of BNSS) to regulate designated protest sites.
