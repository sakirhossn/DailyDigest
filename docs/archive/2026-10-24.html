Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) Statutory Framework

  • Established under Section 45ZB of the amended RBI Act, 1934, following the 2016 statutory amendments.
  • Composed of 6 members: the RBI Governor (Chairperson), the Deputy Governor in charge of monetary policy, one RBI officer, and three external members appointed by the Central Government.
  • Each member has one vote, with the Governor holding a casting vote in the event of a tie; meetings are required at least four times a year with a quorum of four members.

📌 Purchasing Managers' Index (PMI) Methodology

  • A survey-based macroeconomic indicator assessing business conditions across manufacturing and services sectors, compiled globally by S&P Global.
  • The index is calculated on a scale of 0 to 100, where a baseline reading of 50 indicates neutral conditions (no change), above 50 indicates expansion, and below 50 indicates contraction.
  • Key variables evaluated include new orders, output levels, employment trends, suppliers' delivery times, and stocks of purchases.

📌 Minor Forest Produce (MFP) & TRIFED Institutional Architecture

  • Minor Forest Produce (MFP) includes all non-timber forest produce of plant origin under the Forest Rights Act (FRA), 2006, and the PESA Act, 1996.
  • The Tribal Co-operative Marketing Development Federation of India (TRIFED) was established in 1987 under the Multi-State Co-operative Societies Act to support tribal commerce.
  • The Ministry of Tribal Affairs oversees the 'Mechanism for Marketing of Minor Forest Produce (MFP) through Minimum Support Price (MSP) and Development of Value Chain for MFP'.

📌 El Niño-Southern Oscillation (ENSO) System

  • An ocean-atmosphere phenomenon involving periodic temperature fluctuations in the central and eastern equatorial Pacific Ocean.
  • The warm phase (El Niño) weakens the Walker Circulation and is statistically associated with deficient Southwest Monsoon rainfall in India.
  • The cool phase (La Niña) involves anomalous cooling of the central/eastern equatorial Pacific, frequently favouring normal to above-normal Indian monsoon precipitation.

📌 Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI)

  • The apex anti-smuggling agency of India, established on 4 December 1957, functioning under the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), Ministry of Finance.
  • Mandated to enforce provisions of the Customs Act, 1962, and prevent illicit trade in narcotics, foreign currencies, precious metals, and endangered flora and fauna.
  • Operates through regional zones and maintains liaison with international agencies like the World Customs Organization (WCO) and INTERPOL.
