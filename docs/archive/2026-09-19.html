Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act (FCRA)

  • Enacted originally in 1976 and heavily amended in 2010 and 2020, FCRA regulates foreign donations to individuals and associations.
  • Statutory administration rests under the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), requiring mandatory registration and maintenance of an FCRA account at SBI Main Branch, New Delhi.
  • FCRA registration remains valid for 5 years and must be renewed 6 months before expiry.

📌 Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL)

  • Founded originally as Hindustan Aircraft in 1940 by Walchand Hirachand; re-constituted as HAL in 1964 under the Ministry of Defence.
  • Headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, it is categorized as a Navratna Public Sector Undertaking.
  • Key indigenous developments include LCA Tejas, Advanced Light Helicopter (ALH Dhruv), Light Combat Helicopter (Prachand), and HTT-40.

📌 National Mineral Development Corporation (NMDC)

  • Established in 1958 as a premier public sector enterprise, headquartered in Hyderabad, Telangana.
  • Functions under the administrative control of the Union Ministry of Steel as India's largest iron ore producer.
  • Operates major mechanised iron ore mines at Bailadila (Chhattisgarh) and Donimalai (Karnataka).

📌 Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT)

  • Statutory body operating under the Central Board of Revenue Act, 1963.
  • Functions as part of the Department of Revenue within the Ministry of Finance, handling direct tax policies and administration.
  • It consists of a Chairman and six functional members overseeing Income Tax, Corporate Tax, Revenue, and Audit.

📌 Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA)

  • A holistic economic agreement that goes beyond a standard Free Trade Agreement (FTA) by addressing trade in services, investment, regulatory cooperation, and intellectual property.
  • India has active CEPAs with countries such as Japan, South Korea, and the United Arab Emirates (UAE).
  • Framework agreements are overseen by the Department of Commerce under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
