Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India

  • Functions under the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India.
  • Responsible for conducting the decennial Census of India and administering the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969.
  • Headquartered in New Delhi.

📌 Statue of Unity

  • World's tallest statue, standing at 182 meters, located in Kevadia, Gujarat.
  • Dedicated to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, sculpted by Ram V. Sutar.
  • Overlooks the Sardar Sarovar Dam on the Narmada River.

📌 Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC)

  • The monetary policymaking arm of the U.S. Federal Reserve System.
  • Responsible for open market operations and setting the federal funds rate.
  • Consists of 12 voting members, including the Board of Governors and regional Fed presidents.

📌 Maharatna Public Sector Undertakings in India

  • Maharatna status grants enhanced financial and operational autonomy to CPSEs.
  • Criteria include substantial average annual net worth, net profit, and global market presence.
  • Coal India Limited is one of the prominent Maharatna central public sector enterprises.

📌 National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA)

  • A statutory body established under the Wildlife (Protection) Amendment Act of 2006.
  • Operates under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change.
  • Provides overarching oversight for Project Tiger implementation across various tiger reserves in India.
