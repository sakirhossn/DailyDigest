Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Finance Commission of India (Article 280)

  • Constitutional Body: Constituted by the President of India every five years or earlier under Article 280 of the Constitution.
  • Composition: Consists of a Chairman and four other members appointed by the President, with qualifications determined by the Finance Commission Act, 1951.
  • Core Mandate: Recommends the vertical distribution of net tax proceeds between Union and States, horizontal distribution among states, and principles governing Grants-in-Aid under Article 275.

📌 Central Vigilance Commission (CVC)

  • Origin & Mandate: Set up on the recommendations of the Santhanam Committee (1964) and accorded statutory status via the CVC Act, 2003.
  • Composition: Consists of a Central Vigilance Commissioner (Chairperson) and not more than two Vigilance Commissioners.
  • Appointment: Appointed by the President on the recommendation of a three-member committee consisting of the Prime Minister, the Minister of Home Affairs, and the Leader of the Opposition in Lok Sabha.

📌 Section 69A of Information Technology Act, 2000

  • Legal Authority: Empowers the Central Government to block public access to any online information through computer resources.
  • Grounds of Restriction: Sovereignty, integrity, and defence of India, security of the State, friendly relations with foreign states, public order, or preventing incitement to cognizable offences relating to these.
  • Governing Rules: Executed under the Information Technology (Procedure and Safeguards for Blocking for Access of Information by Public) Rules, 2009; designated nodal officer issues orders reviewed by a statutory oversight committee.

📌 The World Bank Group & Poverty Thresholds

  • Headquarters & Foundation: Established alongside the IMF at the Bretton Woods Conference in 1944; headquartered in Washington, D.C.
  • Five Institutions: Comprises IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, and ICSID (India is a member of all except ICSID).
  • Poverty Metrics: International Extreme Poverty Line is set at $2.15 per day, Lower-Middle-Income Line at $3.65/day to $4.20/day, and Upper-Middle-Income Line at $6.85/day (2017 PPP terms).

📌 Indian Air Force Command Structure

  • Operational Commands: IAF operates five operational commands (Western at New Delhi, Eastern at Shillong, Central at Prayagraj, South Western at Gandhinagar, Southern at Thiruvananthapuram).
  • Functional Commands: Operates two functional commands—Training Command (Bengaluru) and Maintenance Command (Nagpur).
  • Supreme Commander: Under Article 53(2) of the Indian Constitution, the President of India is the Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces.
