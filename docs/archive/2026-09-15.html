Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007

  • Enacted to provide for the regulation and supervision of payment systems in India.
  • Designates the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) as the sole authority to regulate payment systems.
  • Empowers RBI to frame regulations regarding Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) and zero-charge frameworks for digital payments like UPI and RuPay.

📌 Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB)

  • Established in 1963 as Special Service Bureau following the 1962 Indo-China War; renamed Sashastra Seema Bal in 2003.
  • Functions under the administrative control of the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA).
  • Mandated to guard India's open international borders with Nepal (1,751 km) and Bhutan (699 km).

📌 Wholesale Price Index (WPI) vs Consumer Price Index (CPI)

  • WPI measures inflation at the producer/wholesale level, published by DPIIT (Base Year: 2011-12) with no services basket.
  • CPI measures inflation at the retail consumer level, published by NSO (Base Year: 2012) and includes services.
  • The RBI officially adopted CPI as its primary inflation metric for monetary policy formulation in 2014 based on the Urjit Patel Committee recommendations.

📌 Open Court Principle & Article 145 of the Indian Constitution

  • Article 145(4) explicitly mandates that no judgment shall be delivered by the Supreme Court save in open court.
  • Open court justice ensures public transparency, judicial accountability, and public trust in legal proceedings.
  • Constitutional Bench exceptions exist for in-camera trials under specific statutory provisions like family law or national security.

📌 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992

  • Inserted Part IX (Articles 243 to 243O) and the 11th Schedule into the Constitution of India.
  • Gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs) creating a 3-tier system: Gram, Intermediate, and District Panchayats.
  • 11th Schedule contains 29 functional items allocated to Panchayats, including education, libraries, and rural infrastructure.
