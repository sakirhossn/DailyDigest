Daily Static GK Boosters

📌 Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) of India

  • Governed by Article 148 to 151 of the Indian Constitution.
  • Appointed by the President of India by warrant under his hand and seal.
  • Holds office for a term of 6 years or until attaining the age of 65 years, whichever is earlier.

📌 Consumer Price Index (CPI) and Inflation Targeting in India

  • CPI (Combined) is released monthly by the National Statistical Office (NSO) under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI).
  • The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) targets retail inflation based on CPI, aiming for a 4% target with a +/- 2% band.
  • Base year for the current CPI series is 2012.

📌 International Monetary Fund (IMF)

  • Established in 1944 at the United Nations Monetary and Financial Conference in Bretton Woods, USA.
  • Headquarters: Washington, D.C., United States.
  • Publishes flagship reports including the World Economic Outlook and Global Financial Stability Report.

📌 Iron Ore Reserves and Production in India

  • India possesses some of the largest iron ore reserves globally, predominantly comprising hematite and magnetite ores.
  • Major producing states include Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Karnataka, and Goa.
  • Hematite is the highest-grade iron ore variety extensively utilized in commercial steel production.

📌 Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS)

  • Introduced by the Reserve Bank of India in 2004 under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999.
  • Permits resident individuals to remit up to a specified USD threshold per financial year for permitted current or capital account transactions.
  • Helps manage capital outflows and foreign exchange reserves stability.
